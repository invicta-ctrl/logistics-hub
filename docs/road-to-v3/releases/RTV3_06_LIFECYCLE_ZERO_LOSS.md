# V2.6 (RTV3-06) — Lifecycle, Recovery & Zero-Loss

**Release identity:** planned product **V2.6**; stable internal milestone **RTV3-06**. Historical V3.x roadmap names are retired. Verify deployment identity before announcing the release; ship required architecture, modularization, and security improvements within this same release (Engineering Constitution §2A).

## Outcome
Complete the logistics physical lifecycle and reconcile late/external reality safely.

## Capabilities
- return;
- damaged/unserviceable state;
- repair/recovery where in scope;
- retirement/disposal record;
- found item;
- missing/late reconciliation;
- Catch-Up Mode for reality recorded after the fact;
- closeout/reconciliation views.

## Rules
- historical ledger/audit truth is not silently rewritten;
- anomalous records remain explainable;
- correction has provenance;
- recovery actions are idempotent.

## AI
May classify/explain a discrepancy or propose a review category.
May not rewrite movement history, inventory truth, or audit history.

## Independence
Lifecycle remains fully operational with predictive features disabled.
