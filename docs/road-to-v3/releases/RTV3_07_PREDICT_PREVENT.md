# V2.7 (RTV3-07) — Predictive & Preventive Operations

**Release identity:** planned product **V2.7**; stable internal milestone **RTV3-07**. Historical V3.x roadmap names are retired. Verify deployment identity before announcing the release; ship required architecture, modularization, and security improvements within this same release (Engineering Constitution §2A).

## Outcome
Use accumulated structured truth to identify preventable shortages, conflicts, and operational risks before they become incidents.

This is **not** the release where AI begins.

AI already exists in V2.0.

## Intelligence hierarchy
1. deterministic rules;
2. descriptive/statistical trends;
3. bounded forecasting/scoring;
4. AI interpretation when measured useful.

## Candidate capabilities
- recurring demand patterns;
- shortage-risk forecast;
- event-overlap conflict prediction;
- reorder timing;
- recurring damage/loss pattern;
- resource bottleneck warnings;
- "likely to become unavailable" explanation.

## Safety
- advisory only;
- evidence visible;
- confidence represented in calibrated/plain language;
- no autonomous approval/purchase/assignment/stock mutation;
- false-positive/false-negative evaluation recorded;
- disable gracefully.

## Independence
V2.0–RTV3-06 operation remains correct when every predictive capability is disabled.
