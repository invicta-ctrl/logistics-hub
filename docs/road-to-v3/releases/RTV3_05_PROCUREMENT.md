# RTV3-05 — Procurement & Replenishment

## Outcome
Close an authorized resource gap from sourcing through receiving into existing stock truth.

## Capabilities
- procurement case;
- supplier records appropriate to scope;
- quote/evidence capture;
- comparison;
- procurement basket/proposal;
- DOL/DOF/outside approval references as accepted;
- packet/document evidence;
- receiving;
- receiving-to-normal inventory/stock movement.

## Security/privacy
- R2 evidence access controlled;
- no secret supplier/private evidence in public surfaces;
- retention category defined;
- approval authority explicit.

## AI
May normalize descriptions, compare bounded quote information, or explain discrepancy.
May not originate a purchase need, approve spending, choose a supplier autonomously, or write stock directly.

## Independence
A procurement case can complete even if RTV3-06 is never built.
