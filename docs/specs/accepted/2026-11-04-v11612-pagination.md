# V1.16.12 Pagination on desktop, easier long lists on phones

Status: **Accepted** by Earl on 2026-11-04 in chat. Parent: V1.16.11 (`main` `72eae19`). Writer: Claude.

Earl's words:
- "implement pagination to the entire website for the desktop, and improve the scrolling in long lists in mobile phones or collapsible module for the phone."
- Choices: **"Numbered pages (Recommended)"**, **"25 (Recommended)"** rows per page, **"Collapsible groups (Recommended)"** on phones.

A phone is a screen under 48rem (768 px), the same breakpoint the rest of the site uses. Turning a tablet across it redraws the list in the other form.

## 1. Desktop: numbered pages of 25

- Under each long list: "‹ Previous 1 2 3 … 9 Next ›", with the range shown, such as "26–50 of 214".
- The current page is filled oxblood. Previous and Next are disabled at the ends. Every target is at least 2.5rem.
- A page's main list keeps its page in the address (`?page=3`), so Back, a reload and a shared link return to it.
- A list beside another on the same page uses its own key: `proposals` and `names` on the catalogue admin page, `templates` on Kits.
- Lists inside a sheet or a group keep their page out of the address.
- A new question starts at page 1: a search, a filter, a sort, a view or a category.
- A record opened by link opens on the page that holds it.
- Turning a page scrolls to the top of the list (instantly under reduced motion). Focus moves to the first row.

## 2. Phones: lists grow by 25, and grouped lists fold

**Ungrouped lists:**
- The first 25 rows show, then "Show 25 more (25 of 214 shown)".
- Focus moves to the first new row.

**Grouped lists** (Lending Hub by category, Self-Service browse by category, Staff Directory by department):
- Each group is a fold: a sticky title with its count, held under the page's own sticky bar.
- An open fold shows its first 5 rows, then "Show all N".
- A search opens every group in full.
- Closing a fold that was scrolled past brings its title back into view.

**Activity:**
- The phone feed keeps loading older entries as it nears its end, up to 300.
- Rows out of sight skip layout and paint (`content-visibility: auto`), so a long feed stays smooth.

## 3. Histories read by cursor: Newer and Older

- The Activity feed's total is not known in advance. On desktop it reads 25 at a time with "‹ Newer" and "Older ›" and the range, such as "26–50 newest first".
- Older past what is loaded fetches the next batch from the Worker first.
- The 300-entry cap and its "narrow the filters or export" note are unchanged.
- The page is not kept in the address, because new entries arrive at the top.
- The Self-Service staff records page the records loaded so far. "Load older records" still fetches the next batch.

## 5. Not paged, and why

- **The Locations tree:** it is a hierarchy, and a page break would cut a branch from its parent.
- **Stock checks:** a check is counted as one sheet.
- **In-sheet histories with "Show older"** (an item's history, a person's activity): short, read by cursor, and already bounded per request.
- **Attention groups while closed:** they already show their first 5.
- **Self-Service attention cards:** each card is work to do, may hold a typed note, and the queue is short.
- **The search palette and home summaries:** already capped by design.

## 6. Not changed

- The Worker, the API, every query and every limit.
- The Worker still bounds each list (for example 100 per Attention reason, 1,000 usage rows, 300 Activity entries).
- What each row shows and does, and the tokens, type and colours.

**Speed:**
- What the browser does for one press (script, style and layout) is measured on the production build.

- How:
  - a page turn replaces only the rows and the pager;
  - a phone's "Show 25 more" adds only the new rows;
  - Items sort with one shared collator;
  - Items cards out of sight on a phone skip layout and paint;
  - item-icon suggestions are worked out once per name.
- This covers the browser only. The Worker and the database are unchanged by this slice, so their times are unchanged.
