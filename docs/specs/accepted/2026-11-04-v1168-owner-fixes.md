## Scope

Each item names the requirement and the files it may change. Nothing else changes.

1. **Rail fold (wide rail).** Items' chevron sits inside the Items plate at its end, as its own button (Items stays a link). It points down while folded and up while open, and each press folds or opens the group. Folding eases out without overshoot, and the rows fade as they go. Group labels (Circulation, Records, Manage) use the same chevron and motion. Files: `src/styles.css` (rail rules only).
2. **No gold crescent on any rail.** The current rail destination has no gold left edge (wide rail) and no gold ring (icon rail). It is marked by lift, lighter fill and top highlight only. This holds for every future rail design. Files: `src/styles.css` (`.rail-lit`).
3. **No "Background removed" note.** A cleaned picture's pedestal shows no badge. The photo control's accessible name still says the background was removed. Files: `src/item-photo.ts`, `src/item-visual.css`.
4. **Centred quantity editor.** The figure sits between its two steps, centred, with its unit and the change on lines of their own beneath. This applies wherever the editor appears (the item sheet and Stock). Files: `src/styles.css` (`.qty-editor*`).
5. **A reason for Individual use.** Individual use needs its specific reason, like USC use, at the desk (Lend) and on the phone (Self-Service). The Worker refuses a desk loan without one ("Enter what it will be used for."). It still accepts a phone borrow without one, because a phone may hold a borrow saved offline before this update. Files: `src/catalog-policy.ts`, `src/loans.ts`, `src/self-service.ts`, `src/loan-form.ts`, `src/self-service-app.ts`.
6. **Home page, centred and improved.** One centred column holds:
   - a small eyebrow naming the University Student Council and Holy Angel University;
   - the heading and lede;
   - the search itself, with Browse the Lending Hub and Show what is available now under it;
   - then the services strip and How borrowing works, with the steps centred.

   The page still makes no catalog request. The copy now mentions choosing an item and Self-Service at the office. Files: `src/public.ts` (`landing()`), `src/styles.css` (`.hero*`, `.section*`, `.steps*`).
7. **Borrowing from the Lending Hub.** `docs/specs/proposed/2026-10-24-lending-hub-borrowing.md` (LH-LEND-01) is accepted at rank 1, A with B, with these defaults for its open decisions:
   - **O1:** A with B.
   - **O2:** no place or hours line. The sheet says only "Come to the Logistics office."
   - **O3:** the hand-off is shown to every visitor while Self-Service is open.
   - **O4:** LH-IP02-S04 is not active, so there is nothing to order.
   - **O5:** online requests and reservations stay with RTV3-02/RTV3-03.

   Every row is one button that opens the item's sheet (picture, count, what it is, how to borrow it). While Self-Service is open and the item is not all out, the sheet offers "Borrow it with Self-Service" (or "Take it with Self-Service") to `/self-service?do=item&item=<id>`. `GET /api/public/catalog` adds `selfService: "open" | "paused"`, and a real change of the setting moves the catalog revision. The open sheet lives in the address as `?item=`.

   Deviations from LH-LEND-01 §3: the hand-off button names the action ("Borrow it with Self-Service") rather than "Open in Self-Service", and the block sits above the in-person steps so the borrow action is the first thing under the item.

   Files: `src/public.ts` (`lending()`), `src/worker.ts` (the public catalog route only), `src/settings.ts` (`setSelfService()`), `src/styles.css` (`.catalogue__*`, `.lend-sheet*`).
8. **A return whose loan was already closed (bug).** A Self-Service return held for checking (RETURN_CHECK) whose own loan was already closed in Loans answered 409 on every confirm, so it could never leave the list.
   - The Worker now clears such a record when staff confirm it. Stock was settled when the loan was closed, so nothing is applied again. It answers `{ resolved: true, alreadyClosed: true }`.
   - The card says the loan was already closed and offers Mark checked.
   - A loan closed while staff were deciding still answers 409, and the list then refreshes itself.

   Files: `src/self-service.ts` (`resolveReview()`), `src/self-service-review.ts`.
9. **Data Privacy Waiver Statement (revision 2).** Every Self-Service form (Borrow, Take, Use and Return) carries the statement, because each one sends a name, a student ID and a photo.
   - The statement's words are Earl's document exactly: the title, the opening paragraph, and Purpose and Scope, Data Collection and Use, Data Protection, and Data Retention and Disposal. The prototype's extra "Consent:" section and contact lines are not in the document, so they are not shown.
   - The form shows the title, the opening paragraph and "Read full statement", which opens the whole statement in a sheet over the form ("Close statement", Escape or the backdrop return to the form, unchanged).
   - Consent is one control, "Yes. I hereby grant consent to the HAU-USC.", unticked on every new form, with "No, I would not give my consent to the HAU-USC." under it. No takes consent back and says the record can't be sent.
   - Until consent is given, the Review button looks as if it is waiting and says so to a screen reader. Pressing it still checks the form and names the first problem; consent is checked last ("Tick “Yes” to give your consent to the Data Privacy Waiver Statement."). Not you? Change clears consent, since the next person gives their own.
   - Check before you send shows "Data privacy: Consent given".
   - **Recorded limit:** consent is required on the phone only. The Worker does not store it, because that needs a migration; a record cannot reach the check without it.

   Files: `src/self-service-app.ts`, `src/self-service.css`, `src/ui.ts` (one icon).
