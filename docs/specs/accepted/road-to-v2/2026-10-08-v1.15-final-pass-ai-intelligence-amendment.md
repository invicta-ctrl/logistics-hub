## 2. Why the original models were inaccurate — measured evidence

The repository's 2026-10-07 leave-one-out experiment used **254 confirmed catalogue fixture items**, with model calls on the 213 eligible difficult cases. The original IBM Granite second opinion produced these non-null precision results:

| Target | Granite accuracy | Why results were insufficient |
|---|---:|---|
| Item behaviour | **47%** | Physical appearance and short names do not establish USC's operational policy |
| Unit | **24%** | Packaging, sale units, storage units and transaction units can differ |
| USC category | **13%** | USC's category names encode local policy, not universal object taxonomy |

The deterministic baseline outperformed Granite whenever it already had reasonable evidence. Granite also tended to answer rather than abstain. Its useful recovery on empty guesses was not sufficient to justify live policy-field suggestions. The vision feasibility check tested **12** sample item photos and Gemma produced a useful short name on **8**, an encouraging but small naming-only test.

**Interpretation:** The model was asked the wrong question with too little organizational context. Do **not** fix this simply by replacing a small LLM with a larger one or routing every item through all four. First fix the task framing, local evidence, known allowed values, missing/unknown handling, and evaluation design.

## 3. Better catalogue intelligence: authoritative evidence pipeline

### 3.1 Separate observation, product concept, and USC policy

A photo may establish *observable properties* (stapler, red packaging, printed brand, twelve individually wrapped pieces). The **product concept** is the normalized kind of item (e.g. stationery/stapler or cleaning-spray bottle). **USC policy fields** are decided by approved catalogue practice (allowed category, Borrow / Take / Use gradually, counting unit, lending eligibility, stock area). Keep these distinct internally even if the UI remains one concise form.

**Evidence precedence (highest first):**

1. The user's current manual edits/confirmed decisions; never overwrite while an inference is in flight.
2. An exact verified existing item record/alias, approved inventory policy, and current approved session/location context.
3. A verified similar-item group with internally consistent classification; reject weak majorities and policy contradictions.
4. Versioned curated item knowledge and packaging rules.
5. Model observations and ranked suggestions that match current allowed values and pass the quality gate.
6. Unknown/needs confirmation, rather than invented certainty.

**Explicit uninferrable facts:** True on-hand count, quantity visible beyond distinguishable units, authoritative location, opening stock, expiry, serial number not legible in the image, approved restock threshold, responsible staff and lending/approval policy cannot be fabricated from appearance. Existing defaults (e.g. quantity 1) are **editable starting values**, not "photo-verified" facts. Location comes from the staff-selected session, not image recognition.

### 3.2 Improve the data before changing the model

Extend existing `src/item-knowledge.ts` (versioned reviewed code/data) and/or its existing item-alias support rather than creating a competing table. At minimum, approved entries should distinguish:

- Product family / type versus USC category; approved USC category IDs or canonical labels, description, inclusion/exclusion rules and 3–10 confirmed examples when available.
- Product terms and packaging terms separately; e.g. `tissue` versus `roll`; `coffee` versus `sachet`; `printer` versus `toner`; `container` versus `contents`.
- Normalized aliases and common brands and Filipino/local terms based on **actual cataloguing usage**; do not hard-code arbitrary speculative translations.
- Counting unit (piece/pack/box/ream/roll etc.) **only with packaging evidence**; do not infer individual pieces from box photographs.
- Behaviour and location/stock-area defaults **only where approved staff policy or strong homogeneous confirmed items support them**.
- Active/inactive status, category and allowed-value validity; no recommendation of non-existent or retired item categories.
- Category-specific ambiguous pairs and examples of when the system must abstain.

**Governance:** Existing item corrections and aliases enter through current authorized audited routes; shared knowledge-base improvements require an approved code review and version bump, plus regression evaluation. No AI output silently becomes policy. If a new editable policy table is truly necessary, justify that against the existing V1.13 no-overlay decision and obtain an explicit schema amendment first.

### 3.3 Grounding and retrieval (reuse V1.11 first)

- Use normalized text search, approved aliases, current item and kit relationships, recent verified items, image hashes and existing duplicate detection to generate a small bounded candidate set.
- Provide models only the minimum **privacy-safe** candidate labels, a few verified exemplars, allowed values and policy distinctions, not the complete D1 row or unrestricted audit history.
- Require candidate IDs from the supplied allowlist. Never accept fabricated item IDs or a new policy class from a model.
- Handle duplicate detection separately: same photograph, matching serial, related product, and a **distinct physical instance** are different cases. Show *Use existing / Save as separate* where warranted; never auto-merge.
- Do not add Vectorize, AI Search or embeddings to V1.15; compare deterministic retrieval first and propose any future retrieval technology only with evidence and a separately accepted change.

### 3.4 Confirmed corrections as the primary learning signal

When a staff member changes a suggestion, distinguish **correcting an error** from **choosing a different valid local convention**. Keep normal, scoped audit evidence and aggregate counts of offered/accepted/corrected fields, preserving privacy. Use repeated staff-confirmed corrections to propose revised aliases/knowledge entries for human review. Avoid feedback loops where an AI-proposed item is later treated as independently verified ground truth before review.

### 3.5 A correction-driven accuracy loop that does not self-poison

1. Capture **only authorized, existing audited item edits** as candidate learning signals; mark whether the old value was manual, model-suggested, approved policy, or a stale session default. The presence of a saved item alone is **not** proof that every AI suggestion was verified.
2. Aggregate recurrent canonical-name, alias, packaging, unit, category and behaviour conflicts without collecting unnecessary personal identities or unbounded raw histories.
3. Present proposed changes to a qualified Catalog/Owner reviewer using existing Administration/Attention patterns. Require explicit approval for local-rule/taxonomy changes; reject contradictory cases or request a policy decision.
4. Version the curated knowledge change, replay negative/positive fixtures, and roll back the knowledge revision if it harms a protected category or creates wrong-confident suggestions.
5. Use the revised knowledge and up-to-date matching exemplars in the next inference; **no fine-tuning, self-modifying prompts, automatic policy changes, or undisclosed external dataset upload** in this release.

**Accuracy-first diagnosis order:** data-label quality → approved taxonomy/allowed values → candidate retrieval → prompt with positive/negative examples → structured output and abstention → head-to-head model swap **only if these simpler changes cannot meet the acceptance criteria**. Store the evaluation method and fixture hashes in the release evidence.

Retain **one server-side Workers AI inference boundary** (currently `env.AI`, `src/ambient-assist.ts`). The **four named Cloudflare-hosted models are the required initial integrations**, not aspirational example entries: implementation must provide a valid task-specific invocation, schema/parser, test double, production availability check, error path and aggregate telemetry for **each**. All four must pass real, privacy-safe smoke calls on the target Cloudflare Free account (or yield a visibly **BLOCKED** release, never a silent downgrade). They share one typed dispatcher, rather than four disconnected systems or staff UI surfaces. Only **per-item inference** is conditional. Revalidate model IDs, supported formats and eligibility at deployment:

| Model candidate (2026-10-07 repo-account listing) | Narrow allowed task | Trigger | Must pass before enabled |
|---|---|---|---|
| `@cf/google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it` | Describe observable catalogue item facts from an intentional catalogue-only photo, not just name | Once per photo revision if online | Per-field held-out visual accuracy; robust structured output; conservative abstention |
| `@cf/ibm-granite/granite-4.0-h-micro` | Normalize aliases/short text to **existing allowed values** | Only when deterministic normalization fails | Must improve over the no-Granite baseline; previous classification test **failed** |

| `@cf/zai-org/glm-4.7-flash` | Rare bounded hard-case interpretation or second independent check | Only a meaningful unresolved ambiguity and ample quota | Demonstrated incremental improvement versus stopping/asking the user |

**Four-slot contractual architecture:** `(VISION_EXTRACT, TEXT_NORMALIZE, CANDIDATE_ARBITRATE, RARE_SECOND_OPINION)` is mandatory and the initial provider mapping is exactly Gemma, Granite, Qwen, GLM respectively. A model can be *wired and live-testable* without being trusted to change a saved field. If it fails the quality bar, keep its tested adapter and route in `SHADOW_EVALUATION`/`ABSTAIN` until fixed; **do not remove its slot, pass a stub as functional, silently enable bad suggestions, or claim the whole capability is quality-certified**. Replace a specific primary model only after like-for-like benchmark evidence, Free-plan/provider verification, privacy review and **explicit owner approval**; the four roles and their test matrix remain intact. The old Granite policy classification failure is **not** a pass for its new normalization job. A model replacement is not a fifth mandatory routine call.

**Routing policy:**

```text
1 Staff captures photo
  -> local context and deterministic catalogue search (0 AI)
  -> Gemma for photo facts only when eligible and not already resolved
  -> deterministic normalization and USC policy mapping
  -> Granite only for proven-useful alias/short-text normalization
  -> Qwen only for unresolved valid candidate ambiguity
  -> GLM only for rare high-value hard case, otherwise ask staff
  -> compose one editable draft; staff validates; normal Save & next
```

Normal cases target **at most one model call**. Most ambiguity cases target **at most two calls**. A rare third call is separately justified. **Zero** calls is valid when a verified exact match resolves the draft; **all four** are exercised only in bounded conformance/benchmark tests, not chained for routine operation. Never auto-chain all four, especially not to create the appearance of sophisticated intelligence. No inference during page loads, ordinary search typing, self-service, account lookup, staff directory or stock/loan transactions.

**Prompting rules:** Small task-specific schemas, typed allowlists, few relevant *verified* examples, forced null/abstain on unknown, negative examples from USC policy, stable temperature/response constraints where supported, and strict output validation. Use independent model/version configuration and failures. Treat labels inside pictures as untrusted task data; they must not instruct the model or Worker to perform an action.

### 5.2 Work-sharing and quality control

- **Gemma** reads the **original** intentionally captured catalogue thumbnail, extracts observable `product_name`, `printed_brand`, `visible_model`, `visible_packaging`, `visible_text` (only where legible), and `uncertain_or_multiple_objects`; never infers USC operational policy directly.
- **Granite** gets the observed name and only the allowed aliases/canonical candidate terms. It returns an existing canonical term/ID or `null`, not an invented category. Run no live normalization suggestions unless its held-out test beats deterministic-only normalization; maintain the adapter and use controlled shadow tests otherwise.
- **Qwen** is used only if *two or more* valid grounded candidate records or allowed workflow choices remain and adjudication **improves the human's review**, not to make an unreviewed policy decision. Its output must select only supplied candidate IDs or `null`.
- **GLM** is a capped, independent interpretation for **rare cases where another answer would change a useful review outcome**. It may identify conflicting evidence or recommend which follow-up confirmation to request; it cannot override staff, policy, stock, permissions or a verified record.
- **Composer** rechecks the current catalogue revision and prioritizes user edits and verified USC policy above every model result. It emits one evidence-grounded `CatalogDraft` with field-level provenance and stale-result cancellation. Saving is solely through the current audited endpoints.

**When accuracy is low, the four-model infrastructure stays.** Remediate data and prompts, benchmark replacement candidates, route to human confirmation, or use bounded shadow tests. A silent switch to a paid model, falsely marking the task done, or auto-filling a worse guess is prohibited.

## 6. Background removal and polished item images

**Goal:** Automatically offer a cleaner-looking product image from an eligible catalogue photograph, without fabricating details or making staff perform a separate process.

### 6.1 Critical technology distinction

**Verified Cloudflare-native first choice (official docs checked 2026-10-08):** Cloudflare Images supports `segment=foreground`, powered by **BiRefNet through Workers AI**, for foreground isolation and transparency; `cf: { image: { segment: "foreground" } }` is the documented Worker transformation option. This is **not a Gemma function** and is separate from the four catalogue reasoning roles. See [Cloudflare Images features](https://developers.cloudflare.com/images/optimization/features/#segment). Cloudflare Images Free covers **5,000 unique transformations per calendar month** for external/R2-stored images, after which new transformations fail rather than automatically incur charges; repeated same-source/same-parameter requests within a month count only once. See [Images pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/images/pricing/). **Crucial remaining account test:** prove segmentation is supported on the target zone/binding/Free plan, establish whether BiRefNet-backed segmentation also affects the account's Workers AI Neuron allocation, and record actual errors/latency/processing outcomes; published general quotas alone do not prove no cross-meter impact. Do not promise zero cost if the account has paid billing enabled.

**Required integration deliverable:** implement a source-preserving, permission-scoped, feature-gated **Cloudflare Images segmentation adapter** in the existing media flow; provide a reproducible non-production end-to-end cutout proof on a permitted catalogue photo. If the provider or free quota cannot deliver it, mark **CLEANUP BLOCKED**, preserve originals and staff workflow, and seek an explicit owner scope decision. Do **not** quietly replace the requirement with basic crop/resize and label the project fully complete. Avoid external paid SaaS/image generation altogether.

### 6.2 Non-destructive pipeline

1. Use **only deliberate inventory catalogue photos**; exclude Staff Directory, faces/IDs, Self-Service, borrower proof and evidence buckets.
2. Preserve the uploaded original/accepted canonical display and thumbnail in governed R2 as currently required, with their privacy and metadata rules.
3. Run bounded object segmentation on a derived copy; retain original shape, labels, proportions and all visible accessories important for identification. Do not generatively invent missing object parts, logos, text or serials.
4. Derive a transparency-preserving asset (if appropriate) and a neutral/background-composited product thumbnail with centered consistent padding, accessible contrast and responsive size.
5. Verify foreground integrity, matting edges, text/product loss and ambiguous multi-object input. If unsuitable, automatically use the original/cropped preview instead. **Preserve source image for vision/OCR-like visual reasoning**: cleaned cutouts may remove text and packaging evidence.
6. Provide a simple **Original / Cleaned** choice only when a valid alternative exists; never replace the source. Keep derived objects traceable to source photo version, avoid duplicate processing, and document cleanup and storage lifecycle.
7. Image cleanup runs independently of item Save and all four text/vision routes. It must not block offline capture or create new paid obligations without approval. Keep private R2 sources and transforms behind authenticated Worker authorization, no public guessable original URLs or provider payload logs, carefully scoped cache keys/response permissions, no public-surface data leak, and no unrelated proof/identity media routing. Confirm transformed assets' format/alpha support and R2 storage/cleanup policy.

**Fallback is not a substitute for completion:** deterministic centering, crop, consistent framing and media compression remain useful when segmentation fails or free transformation allowance is exhausted, but these **must never be represented as actual background removal**. No transformed variant is authoritative evidence; the original remains accessible and deletion/retention follows existing media governance.

## 7. Benchmark and accuracy-improvement acceptance strategy

A different model can help, but no model is declared "better" without a fair held-out comparison. Use existing `npm run evaluate:suggestions -- --ai` evidence as the historic baseline, then extend the evaluation harness to use **the same ground-truth fixtures, candidate records, policy definitions, per-field schemas and allowed options** for every model/prompt variant.

### 7.1 Evaluation dataset and leakage controls

- Reuse the 254 confirmed catalogue fixtures and documented leave-one-out method for text classification; explicitly stratify by policy-sensitive categories, packaging units, ambiguous names, weak/none baseline and rare terms.
- Build a representative, privacy-safe product-photo test set much larger than 12: **target at least 100 independently sourced photos** across office, pantry, medical, equipment, kits, packages, local brands, mixed backgrounds and difficult images, increasing it if rare classes lack coverage. Do not claim statistical certainty from 8/12.
- Human-check ground truth: observable object name, visible printed text, exact verified record association, correct policy class where USC evidence exists, and whether each field is **unknowable**. Unknowable is a valid target.
- Keep photos/variants of the same physical item, and matching catalog records used as demonstrations, in the **same data split**. Never leak an evaluation answer into retrieved examples or prompts. Use a development set for prompt changes and a held-out set for final comparison.
- Do not publish private staff info or raw non-public images to the public repo; use licensed public pictures and non-identifying fixture records for shareable tests. Private production photos need separate explicit privacy authorization and safe storage.

### 7.2 Score outcomes against the existing system

Record per field, category and scenario:

- **Precision of values shown** (among non-abstaining suggestions), coverage/abstention, wrong-confident rate and per-field human correction rate.
- Correct exact item matching, top-3 candidate quality, false duplicate prompts and false negative matches.
- Time and number of staff actions to complete a confirmed record; **compare against deterministic-only current V1.15**.
- p50/p95 latency, fallback rate, model invocation rate, Neurons per useful catalogued item, quota usage and incremental cost per second/field saved.
- Inferred/verified provenance correctness; no field invented from photo outside its evidence class.
- Segmentation visual integrity, source preservation, original fallback frequency, mobile memory, R2 storage/cost.

**Proposed minimum promotion targets (to be confirmed in the accepted spec after fixture review):** name suggestions **at least 90% precision** among shown results; policy-sensitive categories and behaviour **at least 95% precision** among preselected suggestions, with lower-confidence cases abstaining; useful improvement in net correctly completed fields and staff time over the current deterministic baseline; no unacceptable increase in false duplicate suggestions; no quota/latency regression beyond approved bounds. Targets are **quality gates, not promised benchmark outcomes**; if a field/model misses them, return null or staff-review state instead of silently lowering thresholds. Sample size and per-category support must be reported; insufficient data is *unverified*, not a pass.

**Ablation comparisons:** deterministic only → + improved taxonomy/knowledge → + exemplar retrieval → + Gemma field extraction → + Granite normalization → + Qwen arbitration → rare GLM escalation. This establishes which incremental layers truly help. Keep all four **working route adapters**, but only pay for inference when a measured benefit is plausible. A proposed replacement requires the same evaluation protocol plus owner acceptance, never an unexplained silent substitution.

### 7.3 Three explicit gates per model (no misleading pass)

1. **Conformance:** Valid account/Free-plan invocation, correct input mode, well-formed/validated response, bounded costs and failure handling. Required for **all four**; failure blocks four-model completion.
2. **Quality:** On held-out role-specific examples, show statistically meaningful incremental correct suggestions or better review triage against the strengthened no-model baseline. Report sample support, precision, coverage, errors, false-positive rate and statistical uncertainty. **Passing conformance does not mean passing quality.**
3. **Promotion:** Enable model-suggested data in ordinary staff drafts *only* for fields/scenarios that cleared quality + free-budget + privacy/latency tests; all else returns `unknown`, verified local guidance or staff review. The model may remain `SHADOW_EVALUATION` while remediation proceeds, but a role required to materially improve staff prefilling **cannot be silently counted as active**.

**Iteration loop (bounded):** one documented failure taxonomy and at least one focused data/prompt/retrieval improvement pass; rerun split-safe benchmarking; then head-to-head test an eligible alternative if required. If still insufficient, preserve the four-route integration, reject unsafe prefilling and surface an honest partial/blocked-quality release report. Do not lower the precision threshold just to claim all four models are active.

- Requests are authenticated for the staff/catalogue workflow. Only intentional catalogue item images and minimal approved candidate text reach inference. No faces for identification, student/staff IDs, proofs, staff photos, personal notes, audit exports or tokens. Do not route staff-photo uploads into any image cleanup or AI path.

Invisible user-interface mechanics are **not** a reason for hidden personal-data processing.
