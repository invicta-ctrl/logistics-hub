## 2. Product outcome

V1.15 closes Road-to-V2 with a Hub useful without AI, using its free Workers AI allowance only to reduce clerical work and catch online mistakes. AI follows work: staff use ordinary Hub workflows, not an AI page, chat box, prompt, or delegated operational judgment. The Hub supplies the smallest structured current-surface/record/event/item/permission context required to resolve uncertainty.

Local comes first; AI comes second; choose the cheapest capable model; use async work by default; require human confirmation for consequential changes; stay silent when no action needs attention. AI may refine existing operational intent but may not originate it. It does not authorize a generic chatbot, separate AI destination, autonomous Logistics operations, facial recognition, identity-photo analysis, AI-controlled stock/permissions/approvals, or an unbounded AI platform.

## 3. Final model set

At implementation, re-check official Cloudflare documentation for availability, pricing, Free-plan eligibility, deprecation and capability, and record the source date. Unless measurement proves a documented better replacement, use:

Official Cloudflare provider review recorded 2026-10-08 ([pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/pricing/), [Workers AI Gateway](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-gateway/usage/providers/workersai/), [Gateway logging](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-gateway/observability/logging/), [data usage](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/data-usage/)): the four preferred model pages reported availability without a deprecation notice; Gemma supports vision; all four support structured response format; GLM uses `max_completion_tokens` because `max_tokens` is deprecated. The pricing page (updated 2026-10-01) reports a shared 10,000 Neuron daily Free allowance for Free and Paid plans, reset at 00:00 UTC; Paid plans can incur charges above that allowance. Published input/output Neuron rates per million are Granite 1,542/10,158, Qwen 4,625/30,475, Gemma 9,091/27,273 and GLM 5,500/36,400. This is provider capability evidence only: account eligibility, authentication and shared daily usage remain unknown, so the owner-authorized measurement remains gated on quota/authentication and evaluation-harness preflight. Before a future Gateway use, inspect the supported binding types/options; Gateway logging defaults on, so configure payload collection off (or all collection off when needed) and retain no private payload/response analytics. No production configuration is changed by this record.

- [`@cf/ibm-granite/granite-4.0-h-micro`](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/granite-4.0-h-micro/) (Granite) for routine low-cost text: category/unit/behaviour second opinion, normalization, aliases, simple extraction, mapping short human text to allowed values, and low-risk metadata checks.
- [`@cf/google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it/) (Gemma) only for intentional Quick Catalog/catalog item photographs: likely item type, visible product labels, packaging/visual attributes, existing-catalog candidates, and catalog-photo inconsistency. It never receives faces, Staff Directory portraits/scans, official/staff/Student IDs, Self-Service identity proof, borrower proof, transaction evidence, or private ID scans.
- [`@cf/qwen/qwen3-30b-a3b-fp8`](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/qwen3-30b-a3b-fp8/) (Qwen) only when deterministic state has multiple valid choices: rank already-valid fulfilment options, interpret incomplete already-created requests into known fields, select among existing valid choices, or summarize a bounded discrepancy. It never invents stock, requests, approvals, assignments, procurement needs, or intent.
- [`@cf/zai-org/glm-4.7-flash`](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/glm-4.7-flash/) (GLM) only for genuinely unresolved Granite/Qwen ambiguity, difficult multilingual/long text, difficult administrative explanation, or a bounded second interpretation. It is rare and normally first disabled while conserving quota.

## 4. Routing rule

Do not chain all models. Normal deterministic actions use zero AI; most AI-assisted actions use one model; only a small minority use two stages; GLM is rare. After the deterministic core: simple text/classification routes to Granite, catalog-image ambiguity to Gemma, workflow ambiguity to Qwen, and a hard unresolved exception to GLM.

## 5. Ambient Assist UX doctrine

Do not add an AI tab/page, floating Ask AI control, chatbot, prompt box, routine model selector/dashboard, confidence percentage, or AI noise.

- **Level 0:** when AI agrees and changes no next action, show nothing: no badge, toast or model name.
- **Level 1:** put a low-risk useful suggestion beside its existing field, such as a suggested other name, possible existing item, or current-form classification.
- **Level 2:** put discrepancies needing a person into existing Attention with a clear human reason, never an AI Inbox.
- **Level 3:** present higher-impact corrections as concise evidence-grounded before/after proposals with explicit review controls, leading with system evidence rather than “AI says”.

## 6. Context without repeated staff prompting

Construct a bounded structured context from the active workflow: surface, operation, resource, requested amount, available stock, approved substitutes, staff role, and an explicit task such as ranking valid existing options. Models may interpret/rank options already validated by deterministic code; they may not create intent the user did not express.

## 7. Quick Catalog online

Quick Catalog/Rapid Catalogue remains fast and mobile first. After a staff-selected intentional catalog image, acknowledge it immediately; run existing image/hash duplicate, catalog exact/alias, built-in knowledge and deterministic suggestions first; then, only if unresolved, route constrained candidate work to Gemma and use Granite only to normalize short interpretation into live allowed category/unit/behaviour values. Search the current catalog; prefill only bounded/reviewable fields; distinguish inference from confirmed entry; require staff review/correction before normal authoritative save/audit.

If image/name strongly suggests an existing record, offer **Possible existing item** with Use existing or Keep as new. Never silently merge, overwrite, or create a duplicate because of AI uncertainty. AI-filled fields are not human-confirmed until review/acceptance unless an accepted deterministic low-risk default already applies.

## 8. Quick Catalog offline

Offline cataloguing remains fully functional and never waits for AI. Store/queue the intentional photo through existing media handling; run only already-available local deterministic suggestions; let staff enter enough data and save normally. Persist a small structured enrichment intent, never a stale prompt: kind, reason, local/sync record id, queued catalog-media reference, creation time and normal priority.

After normal record/media synchronization: establish idempotency/current server state; re-read current catalog and record; cancel if another user or deterministic evidence resolved it; otherwise select the cheapest capable model; validate against live allowed values/current state; and classify silent, inline low-risk, or Attention/Admin review. Do not overwrite the original offline entry. Offline intents are reconsideration requests, never queued commands.

## 9. Administrative reconciliation

Use existing Item, Attention and Administration surfaces, not a new AI management flow. Internally classify proposals: A harmless/reversible presentation metadata; B reversible catalog metadata (category/unit/behaviour/other names, explicit review unless an accepted deterministic rule permits otherwise); C operational relationship (duplicate/replacement/alternative, explicit review); D authority/history/identity (permissions, IDs, staff identity, movement/loan truth, audit events: explanation only); and E prohibited autonomous action (inventory movement/correction, approval, procurement authorization, deletion/history rewrite, permissions: no execution/auto-approval).

Evidence priority is deterministic invariant, confirmed catalog knowledge, recorded operational/history evidence, then AI interpretation. Group equivalent review findings rather than producing noisy cards; low-risk changes are batch review, never unconditional batch AI fixes.

## 10. Authoritative-write boundary

Models never execute SQL or receive privileged write tools. They return constrained structured suggestions, including expected-current conditions where relevant. Before normal domain action, the Hub re-reads state, checks stale/current conditions, permission and legal transition, requires applicable review, uses the existing endpoint, and produces the normal audit record. No hidden mutation path is permitted.

## 11. Human corrections reduce future calls

Do not fine-tune in V1.15. Repeated confirmed alias/category/behaviour corrections should enter the existing deterministic knowledge architecture through its existing human-confirmed governance. Never automatically promote an AI output into deterministic rules. Familiar work should consume fewer AI calls over time.

## 12. Gateway and provider architecture

Use a Worker binding; never expose AI credentials to browser code. Use one production Cloudflare AI Gateway common control point only if current architecture/support makes it simple; a separate evaluation/test gateway is optional. Do not create a gateway per model; do not add AI Search, Vectorize, embeddings, or a second operational truth. Keep provider observability to real evidence and log aggregate counts, task/outcome/latency/error classes, model ID and available quota accounting, never private prompts/responses for analytics.

## 13. Neuron budget and degradation

Re-verify current shared free allowance. Policy: 8,000 Neurons/day is normal target; 8,000–9,500 is borrowable reserve; 9,500–10,000 is protected, and no intentional operation may enter it. Measure actual use; when precise live usage is unavailable, derive honestly labelled conservative request limits from measured upper bounds.

- 0–6,500: all approved capability.
- 6,500–8,000: conserve; prefer Granite, avoid optional summaries/speculative enrichment and unnecessary vision detail.
- 8,000–9,000: high-value user-triggered/necessary reconciliation; Gemma/Qwen only when needed; GLM exceptional.
- 9,000–9,500: high-value user-triggered work only; stop background/bulk/optional work; GLM disabled unless Owner explicitly invokes a bounded diagnostic/review.
- at or above 9,500: circuit breaker; no further optional AI calls; deterministic/manual Hub operation continues.

## 15. Privacy boundary

Send only minimum task-specific typed item text, approved aliases, live allowed values, bounded catalog candidate names/IDs, intentional catalog images and non-personal product-label text, or bounded deterministic-valid operational options. Never send Student/staff IDs, Staff Directory scans/portraits, faces for identification, Self-Service or borrower proof, transaction evidence, private notes with personal data, full audit exports, unrestricted database rows, passwords, tokens or secrets. No facial recognition or biometric inference.

## 16. Performance doctrine

AI must not slow ordinary navigation or authoritative writes. Never invoke it on page load, route transition, normal search keystrokes, opening sheets, inventory/stock/permissions/Home/Attention/Directory/loan calculation. It may run after an eligible user action, after normal offline sync, in a bounded delta reconciliation of new/changed/unresolved records, or from an explicit context-specific Assist/review action that needs it. Normal save returns through the ordinary endpoint; optional enrichment happens separately without replacing content with spinners or adding polling amplification.

## 17. Bounded reconciliation

Reconcile only deltas: new/changed/unresolved Quick Catalog records, just-synchronized offline entries, or explicit admin selection. Deterministic checks resolve first; only unresolved work reaches the cheapest capable model; validate current state and emit silent/inline/Attention outcome. Do not rescan the catalog on ordinary loads. Valid examples include duplicates, missing classification, inconsistent aliases/units, repeated-return-but-Take, intentional-image mismatch, and offline/current-catalog conflict.

## 18. Quick Catalog acceptance examples

For an online HDMI-to-VGA adapter photo: acknowledge immediately; deterministic checks; Gemma likely class; optional Granite normalization; current-catalog candidate; reviewable prefill; concise existing/new choice; normal audited save; no chatbot.

For an offline unfamiliar black converter: save local photo/manual metadata with no AI, persist enrichment intent, sync record/media first on reconnect, re-read authoritative catalog, then if unresolved use Gemma/Granite and route likely existing-item ambiguity to Attention/Admin. Preserve human entry until authorized review changes it.

## 19. Human-agency rule

AI reduces clerical work, search, repeated entry and recovery cost; staff still express operational intent through normal forms. It may find, compare, normalize, prefill, reconcile, identify inconsistency, explain and rank valid choices. It may not decide event needs, purchases, approvals, assignments, identity, inventory truth, or audit-history deletion/rewriting.

## 22. Data and migration policy

Prefer no migration. If durable quota/breaker/reconciliation state requires one, document exact necessity, use an additive migration, place it in accepted release authority before application, use Cloud Operations preflight/backup/prepare/reconcile, preserve N-1 operability where practical, and provide rollback/recovery evidence. D1 remains structured operational truth; R2 remains governed media/evidence storage; AI-derived state is advisory/rebuildable until a human confirms it through a normal action. Do not introduce a second truth store.

## 27. Definition of done

This amendment is complete only when: the V1.11 measurement ran and is recorded; one router governs approved calls; Granite/Gemma/Qwen/rare GLM have their stated constrained roles; no separate staff AI destination exists; online Quick Catalog provides human-reviewed recognition and offline Quick Catalog remains usable; synchronized entries reconcile against current state; material discrepancies reach existing Attention/Admin; AI cannot mutate authority; human correction improves deterministic knowledge; UI remains calm; V1.14/V1.15 performance holds; 8k/9.5k policy is enforced or conservatively approximated; provider/quota failures leave the Hub operational; state is committed/pushed/handed off; final diff avoids unrelated platform expansion; and V2 evidence is complete/truthful.

> The best AI interaction is often no visible AI interaction. Staff express Logistics intent through Logistics Hub; AI quietly reduces repeated work, finds inconsistencies and prepares better decisions. People remain responsible for consequential decisions; the Hub remains authoritative; AI remains optional, bounded and replaceable.
