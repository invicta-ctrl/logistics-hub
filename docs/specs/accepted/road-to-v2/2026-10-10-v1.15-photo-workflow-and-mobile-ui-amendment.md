## Scope

Item photos must show a complete, unclipped thumbnail. A staff member may take a camera photo or choose a device photo (JPEG, PNG or WebP), prepare a crop with accessible touch and keyboard controls, then Apply, Reset or Cancel it. A crop keeps the sanitized, uncropped 1280 display and its current hash; it derives only the 320 thumbnail. Saving uses the existing expected-photo and updated-at CAS under a new media id. Cancel, reset and stale selection leave the current photo unchanged.

The existing cutout path remains owner-controlled. It must explain owner-off, missing-binding and unavailable states without enabling the switch or making a provider call. Pending output remains browser-checked before acceptance; a failure, stale photo or offline path keeps the original. Catalogue and public thumbnails continue to use the original thumbnail in this checkpoint; no 1280 cutout is exposed publicly and no new provider transformation is made.
