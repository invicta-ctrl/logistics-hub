# V1.16.10 DoL Director role

Status: **Accepted** by Earl on 2026-11-04 in chat. Parent: V1.16.9 (`main` `4e407ce`). Writer: Claude.

Earl's words, with a screenshot of Administration > Staff's role menu: "add a new role "DoL Director" that has the same permissions as DoL Staff for now".

## 1. The role

- **Name:** DoL Director. Stored as the access group `DOL_DIRECTOR`.
- **Permissions:** exactly DoL Staff's, for now:
  - the Logistics Hub (items, stock, loans, Self-Service review and activity);
  - no Administration.
- **Kind of account:** a `STAFF` account, like DoL Staff. Every rule that reads the role treats the two alike, so an administrator may manage a Director as they manage DoL Staff.
- **Storage:** the group is kept as a setting (`system_settings`, key `account_access:<id>`), as every department's group already is. There is no schema change and no migration.

## 2. Where it shows

- **The role menu:** in Administration > Staff, and in a person's sign-in in the Staff Directory, DoL Director comes right after DoL Staff.
- **"What each role can do":** a row reads "The same as DoL Staff, for now."
- **The role hint and the tags:**
  - the hint names the Director as a Logistics Hub user;
  - the Director's tag uses DoL Staff's green.
- **Changing a role:**
  - between DoL Staff and DoL Director, the confirmation adds no gain or loss of access;
  - to or from any other role, it says what access is gained or lost.
  - Either way, the change ends that account's sessions, as every role change does.

## 3. Not changed

- What DoL Staff, other departments, officers, administrators and owners may do.
- Who may manage whom.
- Sessions, sign-in, CSRF, the D1 schema.

Separating the Director's permissions from DoL Staff's later is a new specification.

## 4. Acceptance

- A Director's session reports `access: "DOL_DIRECTOR"` and `hub: true`.
- Every Logistics Hub read answers a Director as it answers DoL Staff. Administration answers 403.
- An administrator can create a Director, and move them back to DoL Staff. The group is recorded and audited, and the account's sessions end.
- The role menu lists DoL Director second.
