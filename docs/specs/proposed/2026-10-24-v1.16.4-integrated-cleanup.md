- **R2 Caching:** authenticated JSON stays `no-store`.

  - The service worker never handles `/api/*`.
- **R3 Client caches:** memory only. Never IndexedDB, Cache Storage or localStorage, and never borrower names, Student IDs or evidence. The Loans and Self-Service review data are not cached.

  - Staff stock movements and staff loans never take on-hand below zero.
  - Self-Service keeps its documented shortage recording (`allowShort`, `docs/OFFLINE_SELF_SERVICE.md:126-129`; D4). A negative balance always shows as Out with its shortage, never as anything gentler.
  - Derived states are computed, never stored.
- **R5 Retry safety:** a request key changes only after the server has answered. Failed validation creates nothing: no loan, movement, audit row or orphaned photo.
- **R6 Validation:** the server is the authority. Client and server share one rule module. A submit never clamps, rounds or truncates, and IDs never pass through `Number()`.
- **R7 History:** new identity rules apply only to new records. Existing loans keep reading and closing, and nothing historical is rewritten.
- **R8 Audit:** the ledger and audit log stay append-only, and every write keeps its actor and recorder.
- **R9 Stack and contracts:**
  - HTML/CSS/TypeScript, Worker, D1, R2. No new dependency, no migration (§8), no paid service.
  - **API changes are additive in this release.** Tabs still running the V1.16.3 client keep working until they reload (§5).

### 4.1 Home (`/staff/home`)

Home has four separate groups. Each has a heading and rules between rows, at a controlled density. There is no card farm and no invented time bucket.

1. **Needs attention:** NOW and SOON entries in server order, three per reason. Each row shows:
   - what (the item or task);
   - why it matters;
   - its **urgency basis**;
   - the next action;
   - a direct link to the record.

   The basis is a short phrase the server writes next to the urgency rule in `attention.ts`, so the rule lives in one place. Examples: "Out of stock with a reorder level set", "Due back 2 days ago".

   "View all N" opens the queue filtered to that reason, using the existing `reason` key. N is the reason's true total. A list longer than one page says "first 100 of N" (`PER_REASON`, S01 journey 6).

   There is no assignee in the data, so rows show the **next action** and never an invented owner.
2. **When there is time:** LATER entries, collapsed to reason headings with their totals. Each opens its list.
3. **Next actions:** the person's own open or paused place checks (existing endpoint), linking to `/staff/locations?check=…`. The group is hidden when empty.
4. **Insights:** a closed disclosure.
   - It requests `/api/staff/home/insights` only when opened.
   - It keeps its stated window and evidence.
   - A failure stays inside it, with Retry.
   - The duplicated catalog-completeness card goes.

- **Labels:** "Today" and "This week" are removed; SOON has no date behind it. The values `urgency=NOW|SOON|LATER` and every existing link stay.
- **Counts and requests:** Home takes its counts from the queue answer it already has, with no second summary request. `/api/staff/home` stops reading the cataloguing session, which Home never shows.
- **Usable:** at 320–390 px, at 200% text, on Slow 4G and with Insights failing.

### 4.3 Inventory workflows and statuses

- **The item drawer is the hub.** It shows:
  - identity and photo;
  - on hand versus on loan;
  - the place and how recently it was confirmed;
  - **one primary next action**, chosen by state: Count, Stock in, Lend, Return, Choose place or Classify.
- **A visible "Stock settings" section** in the drawer holds reorder level, stock area (Pantry), expiry and status, with **Add to restock** and **Add to kit** beside them. Nobody needs to go to Stock or Kits for these.
- **One movement action:**
  - A Stock in for an item with an open restock entry offers to close it.
  - The client passes the `reorderId` the movement endpoint already accepts (`inventory.ts:421-427`).
  - The entry closes in the same batch, under the existing "only if the movement was written" guard (`inventory.ts:428-460`).
  - This fixes Attention still saying "already requested" after a Stock in from the drawer.
- **All on loan:**
  - A loanable item with on-hand **exactly 0** and some units on loan shows **All on loan**, never Out. This is computed, never stored.
  - It leaves STOCK_OUT, the Out lists and the stock badge.
  - A negative balance (a Self-Service shortage) stays **Out**, with its shortage.
  - A loanable item with 0 on hand and nothing on loan is Out.
- **One set of stock reasons,** shared by Stock and the Attention inbox:
  - Out, Low, Needs count and Expiring soon, each with a stated urgency.
  - Open units stays on Stock's list.
  - Stock and the inbox show the same counts per reason.
- **Stock views:**
  - The views are Low & out of stock (the default), Restock and Movements.
  - **Pantry** is the same stock list filtered to the Pantry stock area. Its columns are:
    - on hand;
    - minimum (the reorder level);
    - shortage (minimum minus on hand, when positive);
    - **used in the last 30 days** (the sum of posted Use and Stock out movements; no prediction);
    - expiry.
  - Pantry has no write path of its own:
    - Use, Restock and Count post ledger movements.
    - Opening, marking and closing units use the existing open-units records and audit (V1.7, `open-units.ts:23-40`).
- **One expiry window:** a single constant, default **30 days** (D7). It replaces Stock's 14 days (`stock-workspace.ts:22`) and kits' 30 (`kit-policy.ts:18`).
- **Status vocabulary:** each label belongs to one axis, so no tag text maps to two fields.

  | Axis | Labels | Source |
  |---|---|---|
  | Lifecycle | Active / Inactive; merged duplicates show Merged | `status`, `merged_into` |
  | Classification | Unclassified / Classified | item type `NEEDS_REVIEW` |
  | Details check | Needs details check / Checked | `needs_review` |
  | Quantity confidence | Needs count | status `VERIFY`, shown as lifecycle Active plus Needs count |
  | Availability | In stock / Low / Out / All on loan | computed |
  | Restock | Requested / Planned | `reorders` |

  - Public listing is still gated by `needs_review = 0`.
  - The kit component state "Needs review" is replaced by the specific axis that caused it.
- **History:**
  - Drawer History pages back beyond the first 50 entries.
  - The cursor is `before=<occurred_at, id>`, applied to both the movement and audit streams, which are then merged in the existing `HISTORY_ORDER`.
  - The before → after figures are computed server-side from the whole ledger.
  - Activity remains the full log.
- **"Settle it" and the other in-app links** use client routing (`data-route`), not a full page load.

- **Cut-outs:**

### 4.5 Loans: one policy on two surfaces

**What exists today:**
- **Self-Service** (V1.15 identity amendment §E/§F) requires a full name, an 8-digit Student ID and a photo for every action, with no USC exemption. Individual and USC use differ only by the reason. **This already matches Earl's rule.**
  - A USC-only item is refused for a live Individual borrow.
  - A late (offline) one is held for staff review (`self-service.ts:318-338`).
- **Staff loans** (`PRODUCT_REFERENCE.md:123-124`; database CHECK `0014:38-39`) make the Student ID optional for USC use. They accept the office format `^[A-Z0-9][A-Z0-9-]{2,29}$` (`catalog-policy.ts:217`).

**After this release** (D1–D3):
- **Individual and USC differ only in the USC reason**, on both surfaces, with the same field names, required markers, messages and order.
- **Staff USC loans require the Student ID (D1).**
  - The database CHECK already allows this, so no migration is needed.
  - Consequence: the desk cannot lend to anyone who gives no Student ID. That is the same rule Self-Service already applies.
- **Student ID input:**
  - A text field with the numeric keypad.
  - Digits only, exactly 8, leading zeros kept.
  - Pasting `21-000115` normalises to `21000115`.
  - A ninth digit shows "A Student ID has exactly 8 digits", never a silent cut. This replaces §F's `maxlength="8"` and `.slice(0, 8)`.
  - Full-width digits from an input method are normalised (NFKC) on `compositionend`.
  - The Worker enforces the same rule.
- **For staff loans and the Directory,** the 8-digit rule applies to new records **after Earl re-runs the recorded counts-only check** (D2) and it passes. Until then, staff keeps its current format and Self-Service its 8 digits.
  - The known-borrower suggestions offer only IDs that satisfy the current rule.
- **USC-only items in the staff form** are forced to USC use. The server refuses Individual use for them (D3); a desk loan is live, so there is nothing to hold.
- **Name prefill differs, deliberately:**
  - Staff loans fill the name from the server's known-borrower list.
  - Self-Service fills it only from the phone's own profile (V1.9: no public identity database).
- **Retry safety (R5):**
  - The staff form keeps one request key until the server answers (today it changes on every input, `loan-form.ts:160`).
  - After a lost answer, the next Lend re-sends that key. If the loan exists, the form shows it rather than creating a second.

### 4.6 Numbers: one module, the right domain for each field

**The module.** A pure module, extending `src/catalog-policy.ts` (which already holds `SELF_SERVICE_LIMITS`), is used by both the browser and the Worker. It provides:
- `parseWhole(text, { min, max, signed })`, which returns a value or a coded error: empty, not a number, negative, fraction, exponent, separator, or too large;
- `wholeJson`, for JSON bodies;
- `normalizeStudentId` and `validateStudentId`;
- `validateLoanDraft`;
- one message map shared by both surfaces.

**Inputs.** Quantity fields become `type="text" inputmode="numeric"`, like the movement editor, so garbage can never read back as empty. Steppers may clamp; **a submit never does**.

| Field | Domain | What it fixes |
|---|---|---|
| Staff loan quantity | whole 1 … on shelf | The server parse refuses `1e3`, `0x10`, `+5`, `5.0` (`loans.ts:55`) |
| Self-Service quantity | whole 1–30 | No silent rounding of 2.5 |
| Place-check count, found-here quantity, settle recount | whole 0–100000 | Garbage no longer becomes 0 (`audit-screen.ts:303, 527`). **Today that records a false mismatch or changes stock.** |
| Reorder level (drawer, catalogue) | whole 0–100000 | No silent 0 |
| Restock quantity | whole 1–100000, or an explicit Clear | No silent clear |
| Kit component quantity | whole 1–1000 | Exponents refused |
| Movement editor | signed adjustment / total | Unchanged; it is the reference |

Each wrong input shows its own message. The API returns 400 and writes nothing.

### 4.7 Unsaved edits, everywhere

**One small module** (`src/unsaved.ts`) owns this.
- Sheets and forms register a `dirty` check.
- An untouched or reverted form never prompts, and nothing is stored.
- An in-app **alertdialog** offers **Keep editing** and **Discard**.
  - Keep editing gets focus first, and Escape and an outside click also mean Keep editing.
  - Keep editing changes nothing: values, focus, scroll position and address stay as they were.
- There is no Save draft, because there is no secure draft store. The Catalogue already saves itself.

**API change, named:** `Sheet.discardOk` becomes asynchronous (`Promise<boolean>`). Its nine synchronous callers are converted:
- `staff.ts:486-516`;
- `kits-workspace.ts:178, 362, 391, 435, 533`;
- `locations-workspace.ts:158`;
- the swipe at `ui.ts:428`, which settles the sheet back first and then asks.

**Exit paths guarded while dirty:**
- **Sheet controls:** the X, Cancel, Escape, outside click and the phone swipe.
- **Links:** rail and in-sheet links. `navigate()` asks before it moves.
- **Back and Forward:**
  - Every history entry the app creates carries an index in its `state`.
  - On a `popstate` while dirty, the app restores the address with `history.go(-delta)` and ignores the restoring event.
  - Discard replays `history.go(delta)`.
- **Android system Back:** on an open modal sheet it arrives as `cancel`. If Chrome closes the sheet without a cancellable event, the close handler keeps a dirty sheet's content, reopens it and asks. This is tested on a real Android device.
- **Sign-out.**
- **Tab close or reload:** a `beforeunload` warning, attached only while something is dirty. Its limits are stated in the help and the record: browsers show their own wording, require a prior interaction with the page, and iOS Safari largely ignores it.

**Redirects:**
- Account redirects (must change password, outside the Hub) happen at sign-in, before any form, and do not ask.
- A 401 while a form is dirty shows the dialog first: "Your session ended. Your changes are not saved." with **Stay** (the text stays visible to copy) and **Go to sign in**.

**Covered surfaces** (all unguarded today, or using `window.confirm`):
- Items: edit, add, the Lend tab, quantity, the resolve note and bulk change;
- the Return dialog, which also now closes on navigation;
- Kits; Locations; Loans › Lend; Stock › Update;
- Admin accounts and My account; Admin Catalog names;
- the Directory editor and import;
- Self-Service review notes;
- Self-Service's borrower sheet, whose own guard is folded in.

**Live refreshes** no longer redraw a row that holds a focused or edited form. The Self-Service review note and the Items resolve note keep what was typed.

**Updates:** the build-change reload (§4.8) waits while anything is dirty.

### 4.8 Performance and slow networks

**Server**, in priority order. No migration and no index; none is proven necessary.

A **call** below means one sequential awaited D1 or R2 operation. Calls awaited together in one `Promise.all` count as one.

1. **Fewer sequential calls:**
   - `Promise.all` for the session and inventory reads;
   - `mergedInto` inside the item-detail batch;
   - the cleanup status as one statement;
   - no duplicate `requireCurrentItem` on movements;
   - the five photo HEADs awaited together.

   Budgets: item detail ≤3 calls; inventory ≤3 (≤2 when unchanged); movement write ≤4.
2. **Per-item balances instead of the whole-ledger view:**
   - Applies to item detail, the post-write read-back, kit components, and the code-side write guards (`inventory.ts:452`, `loans.ts:89`).
   - Lists compute balances once.
   - Each figure must equal `inventory_balances.on_hand` for every item, including superseded and imported movements.
   - The database trigger that reads the view (`0017:44-51`) stays as it is. Changing it would need a migration, so it is recorded as a known limit with its measured cost.
3. **Cheaper revalidation:**
   - Activity checks a cheap change probe (the highest event and audit rowids, the revision, and the latest staff display-name change) before running its page query.
   - Insights gets an ETag of revision plus hour.
   - The Self-Service review count becomes a `COUNT`.
   - Staff thumbnails answer 304 from an R2 HEAD before any GET.
4. **Thumbnail caching:**
   - A private `max-age` is allowed only on thumbnail URLs that carry the photo's own identifier. The list already has `photoId`, so a new photo is a new URL.
   - Otherwise `no-cache` stays, so the stale-picture bug V1.16.2 fixed cannot come back.
   - Public thumbnails keep revalidating, so unlisting still works.
5. **Additive contracts only (R9):**
   - Stock still embeds the inventory, and the list keeps its fields, so V1.16.3 tabs keep working.
   - The new client stops re-downloading through the revision cache below.
   - A compact list is added only if WP0 shows production JSON is uncompressed. Then it is opt-in, through a new query parameter.
6. **Measurement:**
   - A `Server-Timing` header (D1 time, R2 time, total) on staff responses.
   - One structured log line per non-304 response and per write: route, status, ms, D1 calls, bytes. No identities and no query text.
   - The volume is checked against the Workers Logs allowance in WP0.

**Client:**
- **Revision cache:** a memory-only cache per account (R3) for the catalog-shaped reads: inventory, stock, locations and kits.
  - A page move draws at once from it and revalidates with `If-None-Match`.
  - The Loans, Kits and Activity pickers reuse the one inventory instead of downloading it again.
  - It is cleared on `forgetSession` and sign-out, on a 401, and on a `BroadcastChannel("staff-session")` sign-out message from another tab. That message also sends the other tab to sign-in.
- **Polling:**
  - Backs off with jitter on failure, doubling up to 5 min.
  - Resets on success, on `online` and when the tab is shown.
  - Keeps the existing pause while hidden.
  - Shows its state truthfully: connecting, updated, not updating, offline, or showing data from a stated time.
- **Cold load:** a cold `/staff/*` load starts the session request alongside the route import.
- **Build-change reload:**
  - The client carries its build identifier.
  - The session answer carries the running one.
  - When they differ, the next page move is a full page load, unless something is dirty.
  - Staff tabs therefore pick up future releases, and later contract changes can stop being additive.
- **Stale versus actionable:**
  - Cached data is shown and labelled as such.
  - Every write (stock, loan, return, approval, evidence) waits for the server and stays idempotent.
  - No write is queued.

**Targets.** Each is checked and reported, and a miss is stated honestly:
- common indexed read p95 ≤350 ms server-side;
- ordinary write p95 ≤500 ms server-side;
- an unchanged revisit is a 304 of ≤1 KB;
- input acknowledgement under 100 ms;
- on Slow 4G, a page move shows its frame at once;
- on Slow 4G, first content and total bytes for Home, Items, item detail and Stock beat the WP0 baseline, cold and warm.
