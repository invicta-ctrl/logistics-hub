## References checked on 2026-10-07 (and what they changed here)

| Source | What it says | Effect here |
|---|---|---|
| web.dev, "Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS)" and "Optimize CLS" | Good is 0.1 or less, poor above 0.25. A shift counts only when something already on screen moves; reserve the space content will take. | Each route was measured on a slow connection. Items (0.25), Kits (0.19) and Administration > Catalog (0.18) were over the line; space is now held for the late parts and every route is 0.10 or less. |
| Material 3 scrollable tabs (Compose `ScrollableTabRow` docs) and Material 2 tabs guidance | Scrollable tabs are the fallback when tabs do not fit; some tabs stay off screen until scrolled. No official rule on edge fades. | The strips keep scrolling sideways at 320 px; nothing is shortened or wrapped onto a second row. A cut label read as a clipping bug ("Returne", "QR c"), so a strip that does not fit now fades on the side that has more. |
| scroll-driven-animations.style "scroll shadows" demo; Chrome and Safari 26 support for `animation-timeline: scroll()`; Firefox status varies | A scroll timeline is active only when the element can scroll, so it can show overflow cues with no script. ## How the evidence was made

The real Worker and D1 ran locally (`npm run test:browser:worker` with throwaway scripts kept out of the repository), seeded by the migrations' public sample catalog and two throwaway accounts. An owner account opened 19 routes at 320, 360, 390, 430, 768 and 1440 px; each page was measured in the browser (sideways scroll, controls off screen, clipped text, targets under 24 px) and photographed. A second pass held every API answer for 2 s at 390 px and photographed each route 0.7 s after it was opened, then measured layout shift once it settled. The pictures were opened and looked at; contact sheets of every route were reviewed at 320 px, and at 768 and 1440 px for the main pages.

## What was seen and fixed

1. **No sideways scroll anywhere.** Not one of the 114 route-and-width combinations scrolled sideways.
2. **The staff bar at 320 px (`phone-320-loans-before.jpg`, `-after.jpg`).** The name was cut to "Logistics H…" and "Staff works…". Below 336 px the mark alone now stands for it; the page title says where you are. At 360 px and up the name fits and stays.
3. **Cut tab labels at 320 px (same images, and `phone-320-self-service-staff-after.jpg`, `phone-320-admin-self-service-after.jpg`).** Loans, Stock, Activity, staff Self-Service and the Administration sections cut their last label mid-word. They now fade on the side that has more.
4. **The Catalog bar at 320 px (`phone-320-catalog-after.jpg`).** "HAU USC LOGI…" was cut beside the Online pill. Below 360 px it shows "Catalog" alone, as Self-Service already does.
5. **Administration > Self-Service (`phone-320-admin-self-service-after.jpg`).** It still told the owner that Take and Use ask for "A name. No photo." and named the database setting. It now states the identity rule (full name, 8-digit student ID number and a photo for every action, a reason for USC use, the condition on return) and only says who changes the switch.
6. **Blank first loads (`phone-390-first-load-placeholder.jpg`).** Opening or refreshing any staff page, the Catalog included, showed an empty screen until the session answered. The bar and the page's shape now show at once.
7. **Layout shift as data arrived.** "Connecting…" turning into "Updated 3:57 AM" re-wrapped the header buttons on Items and Kits; the Items review meter and view strip appeared from nothing; the Catalog coverage placeholder was a third of the real height. Each now holds its space.
8. **The photo name hint (`phone-390-catalog-photo-name.jpg`).** The first version was maroon and bold under the Name field, which reads as a validation error. It is now secondary text with a camera icon, like the suggestion line under it.
9. **Tablet and desktop (`tablet-768-items.jpg`, `desktop-1440-items.jpg`).** Nothing to fix on the main pages: the bar shows every section, the view strip fits, and the table uses the width without stretching.
