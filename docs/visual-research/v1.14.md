## How the evidence was made

The staff pages were drawn by the real Vite app with the API mocked in the browser (6,000 fictional items; nothing real, no Worker, no database), at 1280 px, 390 px at 2x, and 390 px with text enlarged to 200% and reduced motion on. The Self-Service pages used the same method, with the device's storage made to fail as a browser does when it is full (a transaction that aborts with a quota error). A script in the session's scratch area drew them; the images were opened and looked at.

## What was seen and fixed

1. **Items, 6,000 items (`items-show-more-desktop.jpg`, `-phone.jpg`).** Exactly 100 rows, and a "Show 100 more (100 of 6,000 shown)" button under the table. Readable, no sideways scroll.
2. **Items, 200% text on a phone (`items-large-text-reduced-motion-phone.jpg`).** Two defects. (a) The "Show more" button was wider than the screen and cut off at both sides: it now wraps. (b) The on-hand amount ("10 pieces") was drawn on top of the item's name, because the amount never wrapped: on a phone it now wraps and is capped at 8 rem. Re-rendered and checked; no sideways scroll at 200%.
3. **Self-Service, device out of space (`self-service-out-of-space-phone.jpg`).** Before, the confirm sheet said to check for private browsing, which is the wrong advice for a full phone. Now: "This device is out of space, so the record was not saved. Free some space, then save again. What you typed is still here." The sheet keeps its content and its button; pressing it again after freeing space saves the record once (covered by `tests/browser/offline-pressure.spec.ts`).
4. **Self-Service, saved while offline (`self-service-saved-waiting-phone.jpg`).** Unchanged from V1.9: "Offline · 1 waiting" in the header and "Saved on this phone. It will send when you're back online." with its reference. Looked at to confirm that the degraded state still reads clearly.

## Not captured
