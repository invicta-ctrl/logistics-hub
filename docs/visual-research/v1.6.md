# V1.6 visual research and evidence: Staff Catalog PWA & offline cataloguing

Spec: `docs/specs/accepted/road-to-v2/v1.6-catalog-pwa.md`. Engineering: `docs/OFFLINE_CATALOGUE.md`. Screenshots are in `docs/visual-research/v1.6/`. Everything in them is fictional: a demo staff account (Sam Cruz), a place made for the shot (Store room › Shelf B), items saved through the real screens ("Extension reel 10 m", "Folding table" …) and pictures drawn in the browser. The migrated catalog's item names appear because the real seed data is what the throwaway database holds.

## How the evidence was made

- The real application, production build with its service worker, on a throwaway local Worker, D1 and R2 (`wrangler dev --local`), every migration applied (`0001`–`0027`; V1.6 adds none).
- `npm run evidence -- --out docs/visual-research/v1.6 --pages catalog-pwa`. The scene drives Chromium as four devices (each device's screen, touch and user agent): a computer (1440 × 900), an iPhone 15 in a Safari tab, an Android Galaxy Tab S4 and a Pixel 7. **The connection is really cut** (Playwright's offline switch, which the service worker sees too) and the page is reloaded offline.
- Every image was opened and looked at. The first rendered pass found the defects listed below; they were fixed and everything was captured again.
- Measured by the scene (`timings.json`): from **Turn on offline cataloguing** to **Ready** 0.8 s; reopening the Catalogue offline 62 ms (from the saved page); two items (one with a photo) sent 1.8 s after the connection returned. Cold load to the first Items row (683 ms) and the Items → Stock switch (131 ms) are in line with V1.5.
- Installation was checked in Chromium through the DevTools protocol (`Page.getAppManifest`, `Page.getInstallabilityErrors`): the Catalogue links `catalogue.webmanifest` (id `/staff/catalogue`, scope `/staff`), Self-Service links its own (id and scope `/self-service`), and Chromium reports **no installability errors** for either (`tests/worker-browser/worker-v16-catalog-pwa.spec.ts`).
- Not done: real Android and iOS devices, a real Home Screen install on iPhone, a screen reader.

## Patterns adopted, and why they fit Logistics Hub

1. **Readiness is said, not assumed.** One card, **Offline cataloguing on this device**, says **Ready for offline cataloguing**, **Getting ready…**, or exactly what is missing, and until when the device can catalogue offline, with when the catalog was saved. (web.dev; Field Maps' download card.) Nobody walks into the storeroom hoping it works.
2. **Saving offline is the normal path, plainly marked.** The capture screen does not change offline: one note above the form says so ("Offline. Keep going: what you save stays on this device…"), each item says **Not saved yet**, and the bar counts **N waiting to send**, the same words V1.5 used for a dropped connection (one term per concept, amendment §4). Online, saves look done at once. (Expensify, ODK.)
3. **What waits is listed where you start.** The Catalogue page lists what this device has not sent, with only the exceptions spelled out (a photo still to send, one that needs you). Offline it is under a clock, not a second warning. (ODK's Ready to send.)
4. **Finishing offline is allowed and honest.** "3 items catalogued … 3 items are not saved yet. They wait on this device and are sent when you're back online; the session is finished on the server once they are all there."
5. **Signed out is not stuck.** "You're signed out. This device can still catalogue for you until Sun, Oct 11, 9:04 PM, and sends what you save. Sign in again for Items, Stock and the rest." The page shows only what a lease can do.
6. **Install steps are the device's own,** in the platform's current words: Chrome's **Install** button where the browser offers one; Android's **Add to Home screen** › **Install**; iPhone's **⋯** › **Share** › **Add to Home Screen** › **Open as Web App**; a computer's **Cast, save, and share** › **Install Logistics Catalog**. On an iPhone in a Safari tab, the card says that offline cataloguing works from the Home Screen app and does not offer to turn it on in the tab (its storage would not reach the app).
7. **Two apps that look like two apps.** The Catalog's icon is the DOL mark on a cream disc over the staff workspace's dark oxblood, next to Self-Service's cream icon; the Catalog is named "Logistics Catalog" (short name "Catalog").

## Ideas considered and rejected

- **A separate Catalog site (subdomain).** The cleanest isolation, but a production DNS/route change and a second sign-in origin; non-overlapping paths on the one site serve this slice (see `docs/OFFLINE_CATALOGUE.md` §3).
- **Keeping the password (or a token readable by script) on the device to sign in offline.** The spec forbids a reusable password; a script-readable token would widen what a page bug could leak. The lease is an HttpOnly cookie that can only catalogue.
- **Saving the Catalogue's screens on every installed phone.** Every Self-Service phone would download staff code; only devices that turn offline cataloguing on save it.
- **A different word offline ("On this device").** Tried in the first build; it named the same state twice. Removed.
- **Background Sync for the Catalogue.** iOS has none, and cataloguing happens with the app open; sending on save, on reconnect and on return is enough.
- **Offline place creation.** Places are shared staff data with their own rules (depth, active parents); creating them offline would need conflict handling this slice excludes.

## Defects found by looking, and fixed

1. "Ready for offline cataloguing" ran into "Until Sun, Oct 11…" with no break, and "catalog saved" repeated the year: the state is now its own line ("Works without a connection until …. Catalog saved ….").
2. **Turn off** sat alone under the two-line heading on a phone: it is now a quiet link after the status ("Turn off offline cataloguing").
3. Offline, the device card repeated the offline note and offered install steps that need a connection: it is hidden offline.
4. Offline, two stacked amber callouts with the same icon both said "sent when you're back online", and every waiting item repeated "not saved yet": one note; the list shows names, and states only for exceptions, under a clock.
5. The finish summary said "3 items saved" above "3 items are not saved yet": it says "3 items catalogued" while any are on the device.
6. Each waiting row said "Not saved yet" twice (tag and note): the note now gives only the reason ("It will be sent when the connection is back.").
7. The iPhone steps showed Android's vertical **⋮** for Safari's horizontal **⋯** menu; the iPhone card repeated the status line under Install; the install details had double spacing.
8. Signed out on a lease, the page offered **Start cataloguing** although the member's session was open on another device: a lease may now read the Catalogue's own state, and the page offers **Resume** (browser test "signed out, the device keeps cataloguing…").
9. The first build named a waiting capture "On this device" offline and "Not saved yet" online (two terms for one state; found by the V1.5 end-to-end test): one term now.

Found by the end-to-end tests before any screenshot: a session finished offline was reported to the server after a session started after it, so the new one was filed under the old (fixed: finishes go first); and a look-alike typed after an item was sent could not be saved as "a different one" (fixed: the sent item's id is kept).

## Follow-up (2026-10-04): the Catalog installs as itself, and looks like its own app

Earl reported two defects after V1.6 went live: on an iPhone, **Add to Home Screen** from the Catalogue installed a second Self-Service, and the Catalogue (offline above all) wore the staff workspace's bar and sections instead of a look of its own, as Self-Service has.

**Cause of the install defect.** The Catalogue was the one `index.html`, which links Self-Service's manifest; script re-pointed `<link rel="manifest">` after load. Chromium follows such a change (the DevTools check above passed), but iOS Safari takes the manifest the page loaded with. web.dev notes that Safari before iOS 15.4 fetched the manifest only when the share sheet opened ([web.dev: Enhancements](https://web.dev/learn/pwa/enhancements)); current Safari reads it with the page, and Earl's iPhone installed the wrong app. **Fix:** the Catalog has its own page (`staff/catalogue.html`) with its manifest, touch icon and `apple-mobile-web-app-title` in the HTML; moving between the two apps loads the other page (`docs/OFFLINE_CATALOGUE.md` §3). The end-to-end test now checks the manifest in the HTML as served, not after script runs.

### What the Catalog's own frame does, and why

1. **Its own bar, no staff sections.** The DOL mark on a cream disc (as on the Catalog's icon) with **Catalog** / HAU USC LOGISTICS, on the dark oxblood of that icon, so the app and its Home Screen icon read as one thing and not as the staff workspace. Offline, the old bottom bar offered Items, Stock and Loans that could not open; they are gone. The staff workspace is one tap away in the account menu when signed in.
2. **A connection light, like Self-Service's pill:** **Online**, **Offline** (amber ring) or **Signed out**. It says only the connection: what waits to send is counted where the work is (the home list and the capture bar), because a first render that also said "3 waiting" in the bar repeated the capture bar's "3 waiting to send" directly below it (Open Health Stack: show status where it matters; amendment §4: say a thing once).
3. **Home has a band and a lifted first card.** The heading, the one-line purpose and the offline or signed-out note sit on the dark band (amber-ringed, not red: offline is a normal way to work here); the first card (resume, start, or what waits) overlaps its foot, white, rounder and lifted, with a full-width primary button for a thumb.
4. **One column, 46 rem at most.** A phone, or a tablet held upright at a shelf; on a computer the column stays readable instead of stretching to the staff workspace's 90 rem.
5. **No dark theme.** Self-Service has one for phones in a dim hall; the Catalog is used in storerooms by staff and keeps one light paper theme (`color-scheme: light`). Revisit if staff ask.

### Defects found by looking at the renders, and fixed

1. The first card slid under the sticky bar, clipping its top: the band now sits outside the sticky header and the column overlaps it.
2. Empty slots (nothing waiting, no device card offline) and margins kept from the staff workspace's flow left large gaps between blocks: the column's grid spaces them alone.
3. "Offline · 3 waiting" in the bar repeated "3 waiting to send" under it: the bar says only the connection.
4. The "waiting on this device" note in amber half over the dark band looked like a warning on a warning: it is white like the cards, with the clock in the waiting colour.
5. The heading and status note had moved outside `<main>`, so **Skip to content** jumped past them (caught by the accessibility test): `<main>` now holds the band and the column.
