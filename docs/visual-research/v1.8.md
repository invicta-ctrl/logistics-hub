# V1.8 visual research and evidence: Kits & structured containers

Spec: `docs/specs/accepted/road-to-v2/v1.8-kits-containers.md`. Engineering: `docs/PRODUCT_REFERENCE.md` ("Kits, and the container/contents pattern"). Screenshots are in `docs/visual-research/v1.8/`. Everything in them is fictional: a demo staff account (Sam Cruz), a store room made for the shot, and six plausible items (sewing thread, needles, chalk, scissors, bandages, gloves) created through the real API.

## How the evidence was made

- The real application, production build, on a throwaway local Worker and D1 (`wrangler dev --local`), every migration applied (`0001`–`0029`).
- `npm run evidence -- --out docs/visual-research/v1.8 --pages kits`. The scene makes a Ready first-aid kit and an incomplete sewing kit (needles short by one), one template, then captures the list, each kit's profile, a new kit made from a template, the template, the check flow with marks and a note, and the completion summary. Phone (390 px), tablet (768 px) and computer (1440 px) where they differ.
- Every image was opened and looked at. Defects found by looking, all fixed and re-rendered:
  1. An item's picture inside every component row floated at the left edge of the sheet over other content, because the Items table positions that picture absolutely. It now sits in the row's own flow (`src/kits.css`).
  2. On a phone, the chosen answer in a check row ("Running low" with its icon) was clipped in a three-column grid. Answers are two by two on a phone.
  3. The scene's own mistake: closing an unsaved sheet with Escape left it open over the next step; the scene reloads instead.
- Measured by the scene (`timings.json`): Check kit to the first row 42–62 ms. Cold load to the first Items row about 270 ms, in line with V1.7.
- Not done: real phones, a screen reader.

## What this changed

- The kit list shows one state word and a small strip of ready components per kit, so a person can scan which kit needs attention without opening it; the profile puts "Needs attention" first and "Everything else" below.
- A check never presents itself as a stock count: the screen says it records what was seen, and the summary repeats "Stock was not changed by this check" with a link to each finding's own item.
- Four plain answers (All there, Running low, Missing, Damaged), each with an icon and a word, reduce the decision to a tap per component; a note is optional.
