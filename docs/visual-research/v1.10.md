# V1.10 visual research and evidence: Attention

Spec: `docs/specs/accepted/road-to-v2/v1.10-attention-automation.md`. Product: `docs/PRODUCT_REFERENCE.md` ("Attention"). Release record: `docs/road-to-v2/releases/v1.10.md`. Screenshots are in `docs/visual-research/v1.10/`, named `attention-<scene>-<size>` with `desktop` 1440 px, `tablet` 768 px and `phone` 390 px. `timings-before.json` is V1.9 (`a4e0d9e`), `timings-after.json` is this slice.

## How the evidence was made

- The real application, production build, on a throwaway local Worker and D1 (`wrangler dev --local`), every migration applied (`0001`–`0029`), the demo catalog the migrations seed (398 records, most still Unclassified).
- `npm run evidence -- --out <dir> --base a4e0d9e --pages items,stock,loans,attention`. The `attention` scene (`attentionScenes`, with `attentionRecordsSql`) seeds mixed conditions on fictional people: three overdue loans, a damaged and a lost return, three items below their level and one gone, two unsettled check findings, an item reported twice, a phone report, a phone record held for two days, and an Unclassified item whose name resembles two confirmed ones. It then captures, signed in as a staff member (not an owner), the inbox at three sizes (first screen and full page), a filtered view, one group opened, the phone filters opened, a return marked reviewed, a loan opened from its entry, an Unclassified item's Details, and the empty state.
- The empty state is the same page with the two Attention answers emptied in the browser for that picture only, because the demo database cannot be cleared.
- Every image was opened and looked at. Defects found by looking, all fixed and re-rendered:
  1. **The phone's three filters stacked above the list** and pushed the first entry about 400 px down. They fold behind one **Filters (n)** button, the pattern Items already uses.
  2. **The page said "416 entries" next to a bell that said 22.** The two numbers disagreed in everyone's eyes even though both were right (the bell counts only what needs a person now or this week). The page now says **21 to act on · 394 when there is time**, from true totals by urgency the server returns for each group, so it matches the bell.
  3. **"Show all 100" sat under a group of 141**, and "Showing the oldest 100" described a list that is sorted by name. They now read **Show the first 100** and **Showing the first 100 of 141**.
  4. **A loan opened from its entry was marked only by a thin bar**, almost invisible among overdue rows that are already pink. It has a 2 px outline in the brand colour.
  5. **On a phone the suggestion's icon sat alone on a line** and "Open the loan" was indented from the text above it. The suggestion is a two-column grid, and the ghost action has no left padding on a phone.
  6. **"Maybe: Take in Pantry"** read as if Take happened in the Pantry. It now reads "Maybe: Take, category Pantry. Like “Bond Paper - A4” and 2 more."
  7. The scene's own mistakes: a dark picture identical to the light one (the staff workspace has no dark theme; Self-Service does), so it was removed; the Details picture was taken below the suggestion, so the scene scrolls to it; and the demo catalog has no look-alikes for the item it first picked, so the scene seeds one.
- Calm, checked by eye: no row has an alarm background (the loans page's own overdue rows are the only pink in the evidence); urgency is a quiet band heading ("Today", "This week", "When there is time") and one dot in the group's tag; the 394 routine entries are listed but never counted in the bell or in the Loans and Stock numbers; five entries show per group with **Show the first N**.
- Measured by the scene on a throwaway Worker (read the shape, not the absolute figure): the first Attention row draws in 0.23–0.36 s on a cold open, at all three sizes. Navigation, the thing the spec says must not slow, did not move: a cold load to the first Items row took 452 ms before and 461 and 433 ms in two runs after; an Items → Stock switch took 99 ms before and 91 and 89 ms after (medians of seven). The query cost at volume is in the release record.
- Not done: real phones, a screen reader, a Windows run, and a review by anyone but the author.

## Sources (accessed 2026-10-07)

Read through search-result summaries in this session; I did not open the full pages.

| Source | What I took from it |
|---|---|
| [Zurich Design System: badge guidelines](https://zds.zurich.com/0.4.2/atoms/badge__guidelines), [Eleken: badge UI design](https://www.eleken.co/blog-posts/badge-ui-design) | A count badge says how many; above 99 it says **99+**; colour alone is never the message, so it carries hidden text for assistive technology. The bell and the Loans and Stock numbers cap at 99+, hide the digits from screen readers and say "3 need attention" instead. |
| [Carbon: notification pattern](https://carbondesignsystem.com/patterns/notification-pattern) | Notifications should be relevant and as undisruptive as possible; an actionable one stays until its action is taken. An Attention entry stays until its cause is fixed and carries its action; a toast appears only to confirm what the person just did ("Marked as reviewed"). |
| [Nielsen Norman Group: empty states in complex applications](https://nngroup.com/articles/empty-state-interface-design), [setproduct: empty state UI](https://www.setproduct.com/blog/empty-state-ui-design) | An empty state reports status, says what belongs there and shows the next step; a finished task should not look like "No items" with a sad face. The empty page says **Nothing needs attention**, what that means for loans, stock and records, and that the page fills itself in. |
| [W3C: Understanding Target Size (Minimum) 2.5.8](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) | Pointer targets are at least 24 × 24 CSS px, or spaced. The bell is 44 px; rows, selects and buttons use the app's existing targets (at least 40 px). |
| [Linear: Inbox](https://linear.app/docs/inbox), [Linear: Triage](https://linear.app/docs/triage) | An inbox is for what needs the person; items leave it by being dealt with, with a deliberate way to defer. Kept: it holds only actionable work (passive history stays in Activity). Not copied: snooze and delete. The spec forbids a status people maintain, so entries leave only when the cause is fixed, and the single manual act, **Mark reviewed** on a damaged or lost return, is recorded. |
| [GitHub: triaging notifications](https://docs.github.com/en/enterprise-cloud@latest/subscriptions-and-notifications/tutorials/customizing-a-workflow-for-triaging-your-notifications) | Filtering by the reason a notification exists is how people triage a long list. The filters are **From** (the source), **How soon** and **Age**, and every group is headed by its reason in words. |
| [PagerDuty: reducing noise](https://www.pagerduty.com/ops-guides/ops-practices/reduce-noise/) | Urgency is set by a stated rule per kind of event, and low urgency does not escalate or interrupt; alert fatigue is the failure to avoid. Each reason has one fixed urgency (see the release record), there is no score, and routine entries are never counted in the shell. |

## What this means for Logistics Hub

Staff are a few people with other work. The page therefore answers one question, "what needs me, and where do I go", groups by the reason in plain words, keeps routine work visible but quiet, and never asks anyone to manage the list.
