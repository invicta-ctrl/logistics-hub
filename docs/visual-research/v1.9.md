Spec: `docs/specs/accepted/road-to-v2/v1.9-self-service-2.md`. Engineering: `docs/OFFLINE_SELF_SERVICE.md` §17 and `docs/PRODUCT_REFERENCE.md` ("Self-Service"). Screenshots are in `docs/visual-research/v1.9/` (`before-*` are V1.8; `ss-*` are V1.9, suffixed by size: `phone` 390 px, `narrow` 320 px, `tablet` 768 px, `desktop` 1440 px). Everything in them is fictional: Maria Santos and the made-up ID `20-1234-567` are typed by the scene, and the "photo" is a shape drawn in the browser, so no real person, ID or photograph is in the repository.

## How the evidence was made

- The real application, production build, on a throwaway local Worker and D1 (`wrangler dev --local`), every migration applied (`0001`–`0029`), the demo catalog the migrations seed.
- `npm run evidence -- --out docs/visual-research/v1.9 --base HEAD --pages self-service-v19`. The scene (`selfServiceScenes` in `scripts/visual-evidence.mjs`) is signed out, as a phone is: home, search, a group, an item to borrow and one to take, Where is it?, a full borrow (the form, the first problem in words, the photo preview, the check, the receipt), My activity with the loan and the waiting record, the return form, the take form for a remembered person, the help tip opened by keyboard, the item page offline, and a 600-item catalog. Sync is held back so a confirmed record waits on the phone, which is what the receipt and My activity then show.
- Every image was opened and looked at. Defects found by looking, all fixed and re-rendered:
  1. On a wide screen the home stayed a 540 px column of two cards with a lot of empty photograph either side. From 48 rem it is now up to 62 rem wide with four cards across (the search and the two shortcuts stay 36 rem).
  2. The check's title ("Check before you send") sat flush against the sheet's header, and after a failed submit the first invalid field sat under it with its label scrolled away. Focused fields and the title now keep room above them (`scroll-margin-top`).
  3. A reference such as `SS-TCZ3-03K0` wrapped at its hyphen in My activity. It is one token and never breaks.
  4. "Where is it?" and "Not you? Change" were the brand red on the dark canvas, about 3.8 : 1, under the 4.5 : 1 that normal text needs. On the dark theme links are gold.
  5. With "Offline · 1 waiting" the app's name shrank to "Self-Serv…". On a phone the second crest steps aside so the name fits.
  6. "What do you need?" sat over the brightest lit window of the photograph. The dark wash is a little deeper behind the headline and the headline has a soft shadow; the light theme was checked and reads well.
  7. The scene's own mistake: the offline page was photographed during its entrance and looked faded. The scene waits; the page's text colour was read back from the browser (`rgb(247, 236, 225)` on `rgb(20, 6, 9)`).
- Measured by the scene (`v1.9/timings.json`, Chromium on a throwaway Worker, so read the shape): the home draws 8 cards (4 per group, two groups in the seed) in 0.77–0.84 s including the network; a 600-item catalog draws 12 cards in 0.24 s, and opening a 200-item group takes 0.12 s. The unit test and the browser spec (`tests/browser/self-service-v19.spec.ts`) hold the same bounds.
- Not done: real phones, a real camera, a screen reader, a Windows run.

## What this changed

- Home answers "what do you need?" in this order: search, the two things people come back for (Return, My activity), then a few items from each group. The three old equal tiles, including **Get an item**, are gone because search and the groups are the way into an item.
- An item has a page. It says in a sentence what will happen, so the choice between Take, Borrow and Use is never the person's, and it puts **Where is it?** where people look for it.
- A person is asked who they are once. The form shows them as a card, and **Not you? Change** is one tap, so a shared phone does not silently remember the wrong person.
- Nothing is recorded by accident: every action ends in **Check before you send**, and what was sent comes back as a receipt with a reference staff can search.
- Help appears only beside the three terms that need it (the student ID, the photo, remembering you), through one component.
