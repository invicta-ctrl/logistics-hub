# V1.3 visual research and evidence: USC Staff Directory

Spec: `docs/specs/accepted/road-to-v2/v1.3-staff-directory.md` (source amended by `docs/specs/accepted/road-to-v2/2026-10-03-v1.3-google-drive-source-amendment.md`). Screenshots are in `docs/visual-research/v1.3/` (`before/` is `main` at V1.2, `after/` is this branch). Every person in them is fictional, and every ID card is an obviously fake "SAMPLE STUDENT COUNCIL" card drawn by the evidence script on a canvas at run time: a plain silhouette instead of a face, invented numbers, "SAMPLE" across it. No real scan, no image of a real ID and no personal data from the private source appears in this folder or anywhere in Git.

## How the evidence was made

- The real application on a throwaway local Worker, D1 and R2 (`wrangler dev --local`) with every migration applied (`0001`–`0021`), the two fictional demo accounts (Alex Reyes as Owner, Sam Cruz as Staff) and fictional loans and phone takes written the way the app writes them.
- `npm run evidence -- --out docs/visual-research/v1.3 --base origin/main --pages admin,account,staff-directory`. `admin` and `account` render on both refs (before and after compare). `staff-directory` runs only where the directory exists: it adds 24 fictional people (a Department of Logistics of 16, officers in every office, partial profiles with no position, one inactive person), draws the sample archive into a throwaway folder laid out as the Drive archive is (`[DEM] Official ID`, `[DoL] Official ID`, `[DHR] Official ID`, `OFFICERS`, one `desktop.ini`) with one of each problem the preflight reports, imports it **through the owner's real import screen**, and captures desktop (1440×900), tablet (768×1024, 2×) and phone (390×844, 2×). Reduced motion is on for stable frames; the flip and zoom are exercised by keys.
- Every image was opened and looked at, not only asserted on. The first pass found real defects, listed below, which were fixed and captured again. Files are WebP (quality 72); unchanged menus and the tablet copies of the denser screens were dropped (2.4 MB).

## Patterns adopted, and why they fit Logistics Hub

- **Departments as panels of compact rows, in two columns (team directories).** The council has ten offices and departments of very different sizes (one President, a Department of Logistics of 16). The first pass used a card grid per department; with many one-person offices that left one card per row and a long scroll (defect 1). Panels of rows in CSS columns keep every office visible on one desktop screen, and the large department reads as a list. A single department (from the department index) spreads its rows over a grid.
- **Department tints, used only for initials and dots.** One tint and ink per code (ink on tint at least 7:1), so a department is recognisable in the list, the profile header and the import list without adding a new accent to the oxblood interface.
- **Only what is present gets a tag.** Officer, USC ID on file, the linked sign-in and Inactive. "No USC ID" on nearly every row was noise (defect 2); what is missing has its own filter chip with a count instead.
- **Profile as a summary list beside its side panel (GOV.UK).** Details on the left, the sign-in link (or recent ID openings, or the directory record) on the right on wide screens, stacked on phones.
- **Tabs with manual activation (APG).** Profile | USC ID | Usage | Loans | Activity load data, so arrows move and Enter opens; the open section is in the URL, so Back, reload and sharing a link keep it.
- **The card is one object you turn over (document tools).** The viewer shows one card in the middle of a dark stage, Front/Back as a two-state switch, and a short 3D turn between the sides (480 ms; instant under reduced motion). Zoom 1–4× by wheel, pinch, double-click, + / − / Fit, pan by dragging or arrow keys; F and B turn it; Escape and Back close it. The first pass let the page show through the viewer (defect 4); the stage is now opaque, which also keeps the page behind out of view while an ID is open.
- **Who opened it, next to it (EHR access logs).** The USC ID section lists recent openings; the Activity section keeps the directory record (added, edited, linked, imported, opened). Every opening is also in Activity under "Staff Directory", once per viewer and card every ten minutes.
- **Import as a check before an upload.** The owner chooses the downloaded folder; the check runs on the device (names and hashes only) and shows what is ready, what needs a person and why, what is already in the directory and what was skipped, before anything is sent.

## What the screenshots showed, and what changed

1. **One card per row for one-person offices** (list, desktop). Replaced by department panels of compact rows in two columns.
2. **"No USC ID" on almost every card.** Removed; the filter chip carries the count.
3. **Phone list wider than the screen.** The single-column layout used `1fr`, which grew to the width of the scrolling department chips; now `minmax(0, 1fr)`. The accessibility checks now assert no sideways scroll at 320–1366 px and at 200% text.
4. **The page showed through the ID viewer**, and on phones the close button wrapped onto the zoom row. The viewer is opaque; on phones the title and close share the first row, Front/Back and zoom the second.
5. **The chosen department scrolled out of sight** in the phone/tablet chip row. It is scrolled into view.
6. **Usage "Area" label cut off** ("Inventory and |"). Now "All areas".
7. **Activity summaries as grey boxes** were heavy. Now the sentence is the line, with its type and time beneath.
8. **Profile sections a single narrow column on wide screens.** Now details and the side panel sit side by side from 1100 px.

## Measurements (`after/timings.json`, median of runs on the local Worker)

- Opening the viewer from a tile: 57 ms desktop, 72 ms tablet, 71 ms phone (the scan is already in memory from the tile).
- Turning the card: about 105 ms to the frame after the key (the 3D turn itself is off under reduced motion in these runs).
- Importing six sample pairs through the owner screen: 0.86 s, including re-encoding each scan on the device.
- Cold load to the first Items row 282 ms after, 260 ms before; section switch 72 ms after, 66 ms before. Both are within the run-to-run spread of this machine (the directory code is its own lazy chunk; the shell gained only the Administration sub-navigation).
