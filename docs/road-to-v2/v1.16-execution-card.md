## Product outcome

Strong staff correction evidence improves versioned catalogue knowledge through review, not model self-training. Existing Administration > System > This version displays the running artifact's real product version plus a short what's-new summary, separately from build fingerprint, commit, timestamp and migrations.

## Model evaluation matrix
- Gemma visual extraction: real approved catalogue images when available; names, printed identifiers, rejection of non-item pictures and prompt-injection text.
- Granite normalization: short, narrow candidate/alias matching against deterministic baseline.
- Qwen arbitration: ambiguous verified candidate sets, must abstain when evidence insufficient.
- GLM difficult-case: only rare conflicting authorized facts; must beat staff clarification costs and fail quietly.
- Benchmark permitted challenger(s) on exactly the same held-out fixtures. Compare precision, abstention, latency, Neurons, availability and added utility; never switch a trusted role on novelty alone.
- V1.15 REVIEW_ONLY restrictions remain until separate accepted quality gates pass.

## Core degraded-mode matrix
- No AI binding and AI off.
- Daily Neuron app stop and provider 429.
- Model 403/restricted access, 500, timeout, empty/malformed structured output.
- Offline, reconnect/replay, stale source revisions, duplicate tasks, two writers on same item.
- Public and unauthorized staff data access denied.
- Core stock/loan/count saves complete at ordinary latency without awaiting inference.
- Verified manual corrections remain durable, idempotent, auditable and independent of AI.
- Picture cleanup retains its separate Images quota and original-media fallback.
