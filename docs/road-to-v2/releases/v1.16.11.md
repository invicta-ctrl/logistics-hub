## What changed

**Photos** (`src/styles.css`, `src/self-service.css`):
- The Lending Hub sheet's picture frame is the full width at 4:3, up to 24rem. Before, a small tile sat inside a grey stage.
- The pedestal under a cleaned picture is scaled to the frame.
- Self-Service's item picture is the full width at 4:3, up to 26rem. Cards show the picture edge to edge.

**Public page** (`src/public.ts`):
- The header shows the DoL mark on a paper disc instead of the HAU crest.
- The hero's second link was "Show what is available now" (`/lending?available=1`). With nearly everything available it showed the same list as the first. It is now "At the office? Use Self-Service" (`/self-service`).

**Self-Service** (`src/self-service-app.ts`, `src/self-service.css`):
- **Theme:** light by default, with dark only once chosen on that phone. The bar is oxblood in both.
- **Home:**
  - The campus photo is a faint, darkened band behind the bar, heading and search, on home only.
  - Four tiles: Borrow, Take, Return and My activity. Borrow and Take open their group and show only while it is offered.
- **Cards:** the picture runs edge to edge, and each count has a dot for its level.
- **Item page:** the picture, then one details card (type, count, how, where). The action is pinned to the bottom.
- **Forms:** numbered steps, each checked off when complete.
  - The first step is About the loan, Amount or Condition.
  - Then Who you are, A photo, and the Data Privacy Waiver Statement.
  - Borrow asks its reason, amount and return date before identity.
  - Field names, values and validation are unchanged.

**Tests** (`tests/browser/app.spec.ts`):
- The header shows the mark, and no crest.
- The hero's link opens Self-Service.
- Self-Service starts light under the campus band. The band is absent on other screens, and dark is remembered.
- The item picture is as wide as the details card.
