## WP3 item sheet, stock settings, history, Pantry and photos — 2026-10-28 (`30dd630`, `a9732a7`)

- **The item sheet leads with one next step.** It holds Stock settings (reorder level, stock area, expiry, status), which save on their own under the item's version, and Add to restock and Add to kit. A stale save is refused and offers the latest details.
- **A Stock in closes the restock entry it names**, with one movement and one audit entry, in the same D1 batch as `b70a584`'s guard.
- **History pages back** with a `before=<time,id>` cursor over movements and audit. Each movement on a later page carries its balance from the whole ledger. A page marker the Worker did not make is refused.
- **Stock's views** are Low & out of stock, Restock, Movements and Pantry. Pantry shows on hand, the minimum, the shortage below it, 30 days of use and the earliest expiry. None on hand with units on loan is All on loan, not Out; a negative balance shows as Out by how much (§4.3).
- **Kit parts** name their one cause in the kit's detail.
- **Photos (§4.4).** The sheet's tile shows the detail picture, never `/display` or `/cutout`; the full picture is fetched only for View larger. The pedestal is matte. A missing photo is a 4rem icon chip. A picture that will not load falls back to the thumbnail, then to the item's icon. A staff thumbnail keeps a private day's `max-age` only when its address names the photo's version; without one it stays `no-cache` (`a9732a7`: an empty `p` is not a version).
- **Tests.** `tests/v1164-wp3.test.ts` (7 Worker cases: paging end to end with nothing missed or repeated, ledger balances, refused markers and the detail's call budget, settings written alone under the version, the closing Stock in, Pantry use, kit causes). `tests/browser/v1164-wp3.spec.ts` (10 cases: the sheet's next step, settings, the stale save, restock and kit from the sheet, history paging, Stock and Pantry, the detail picture, both fallbacks and the phone's icon chip).

### Large text on a phone (`77b4eef`, `b62dab3`)

The body clips sideways overflow (`overflow-x: clip`), so a page never scrolls sideways and §4.9's rule holds on every page. Whatever runs past the edge is cut off instead, and the earlier checks, which measured only the document's scroll width, could not see it. The WP7 sweep at 320 and 390 px with 200% text therefore also looked for text running out of its own box and past the edge:

- `77b4eef`: Stock's movements had empty time and icon columns before the item, so the sentence had 58 px and the change overlapped the item's name. The rows now hold only the sentence and the change, and desktop loses the empty indent.
- `77b4eef`: a full-width button could not wrap its label and was cut off at both ends. Full-width buttons, a form's own buttons and Attention's "Show the first 100" now wrap.
- `b62dab3`: a long word in a title widened its column past the phone: "maintenance" on Self-Service's closed screen, and "Administration". `77b4eef` had hidden this with `hyphens: auto`, and removing that in `f50d549` brought it back. Titles now break a long word themselves (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`) and need no hyphenation dictionary.
- `b62dab3`: Administration's status rows let a state cover its name ("Closed for maintenance" over "Self-Service"). On phones the state now moves under the name and wraps. Administration's own buttons and its stat strip's labels wrap, and the strip falls to one column once large text needs it; at normal text it keeps its two columns on phones and four from 1024 px.
- The large-text checks in `tests/browser/accessibility.spec.ts` now also measure the body's scroll width, which counts what the clip hides. Run against V1.16.3's source they fail 10 page checks; against the final source, the 3 pre-existing ones named under Known limits.
