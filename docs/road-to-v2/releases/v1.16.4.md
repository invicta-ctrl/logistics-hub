## WP4 navigation and Home — 2026-10-28

- **Items is the parent.** Its six children keep one order at every width: All items, Low & out of stock, Stock & movements, Pantry, Locations and Kits. The wide rail lists them under Items. The tablet's All destinations and the phone's More list them under an Items heading.
- **Current marking.** On a child's page, Items carries `aria-current="true"` and the child shown carries `aria-current="page"`. Stock changes its view in place, so it marks the child again on every view change. Stock, Locations and Kits each lead back up to Items.

- **One request feeds Home.** Home asks only `/api/staff/attention`, and the bar's counts come from the same answer. Neither Home nor Attention calls the summary endpoint.

  - Old Home addresses that carry queue filters redirect to `/staff/attention` with the same filters.

## Instant moves between staff pages (`ab01e92`, `bb68058`)

Earl's direction of 2026-10-24 was: "when selecting different tabs or modules it should be instant and if the requested page needs to load just show the skeleton loading styles".

- **The router draws first.** The page being left lets go at once, the rail marks the destination, and the next page's skeleton shows while its code and data load.
- **The signed-in account is kept for the page's lifetime.** It is re-checked in the background on every move. Sign-out, an ended session or a required password change take effect there. An account that must change its password, or is outside the Hub, is never kept.
- **A sheet closed by leaving a page no longer rewrites the next page's address.** This fixes "All activity for this item" landing on unfiltered Activity.
- **Tests.** Before the merge into this release, `tests/browser/staff-shell.spec.ts` covered both behaviours, and Worker E2E passed 90/90 on `bb680588613786259425ad6c114d2582b5361952`, the source commit `product-release.json` names until the final application source is qualified.
