## What was wrong

Earl reported on 2026-10-29, from production, that switching between Administration's tabs showed "Something went wrong. Please try again." several times, and that the screen flickered on every tab switch.

- **The errors.** A page's reads kept running after the page was left. When one answered, it tried to draw into elements that were already gone, the error went unhandled, and the app's last-resort handler showed the toast.
  - Administration's Staff accounts and Accountability lists did this, and so did opening a kit and loading older Activity.
  - Moving quickly between sections left several such reads behind, hence several toasts.
- **The flicker.** Every move redrew the whole shell: the rail, the top bar and the page's title and tabs.
  - The page's own body was then replaced by a generic skeleton until it loaded.
  - On Administration this blanked the title and section tabs on each switch.

## What changed

- **A page knows when it has been left.** `stillShown()` in `src/ui.ts` answers whether the view that started a read is still the one shown.
  - The session read that every staff view starts with (`loadSession`) answers nothing to a view left while it loaded, so no page is drawn over the next one.
  - Administration (accounts, Accountability, Other names, Self-Service settings), My account, Kits and Activity draw nothing from a read that answers after their page was left. Nothing is reported, because no one is waiting for it.
  - A one-time temporary password or recovery key that could not be shown, because its page was left, is reported as not shown rather than lost silently.
- **The shell stays.** When a move changes only which destination is current, the rail and bar are kept and only their current marks change (`keptChrome` in `src/staff-shell.ts`).
  - Only the page area is replaced. Listeners a page puts on it (Activity's filters, My account's copy buttons) leave with the page.
  - The rail's tooltips and their listeners are rebound per page, and the account menu closes on every move, Back included.
- **Section tabs stay.** Moving between tabs of the same page (Administration's sections, for example) keeps the title and tabs. The new tab is marked at once, and only the area below shows a loading state.
  - The section being left keeps no lede or buttons that still work: they are hidden in place, so nothing moves, until the next section draws its own.
