## What shipped

- **Cataloguing sessions.** Items → **Catalogue** (also `/staff/catalogue`). A session is one signed-in staff member walking a shelf: they choose the place once and it stays chosen. It is stored in D1 (`catalogue_sessions`), so it resumes on any device that person signs in on; one open session per person (the database allows no more), and others can see who else is cataloguing where. Each capture also records its own place, so changing the shelf never rewrites earlier items.
- **Capture → save → next.** One form: photo, name, how it is used, how many, category, unit, with model, serial number, other names, stock area and notes under *More details*. Saving clears the form and puts the cursor back in the name; the record goes to a short queue shown below ("Just added"), so the next item starts at once. Ctrl+Enter saves, Alt+1–4 chooses how it is used, Alt+P opens the camera, Enter moves to the next field and never saves.
- **How it is used is a decision per item** (never a session default): Borrow & return, Consume, Use gradually, or Not sure / Review later. One shelf holds all of them.
- **Suggestions** (`src/catalogue-suggest.ts`, deterministic, no network): from items whose names share words with what is typed (the last word may be half typed), or from the session's own last two items. Each says why ("Like “Whiteboard Marker Black” and 1 more", "Same as your last two items"); the suggested choice is outlined and tagged, nothing is selected or saved, and *Use these* names everything it will fill (including the stock area behind *More details*) before it fills it.
- **Possible duplicates** (`src/duplicates.ts`, shared by the browser and the Worker): same serial number; same name or other name (any word order, case, punctuation, plural); same model in the same category; a photo that looks the same (a 64-bit difference hash made in the browser, stored in `item_media.dhash`); almost the same name (one name inside the other with at most a word more). Two different serial numbers are never twins. Matches are shown as a card with the reason, count and place and a link that opens the item in a new tab; the first press of Save shows it, the second ("Save as a separate item") saves. The Worker re-checks every capture and answers 409 with the matches unless the person has acknowledged them; the acknowledgement is stored with the capture.
- **Review later.** *Not sure* keeps photo, name, place and quantity and makes category and unit optional. The record is stored unclassified (type Unclassified, category `UNSORTED`, unit `piece` as placeholders, needs review, not lendable), so it cannot reach the Lending Hub, the Self-Service catalog, the Self-Service photo route or a phone's location report. It is listed on the Catalogue page with a *Decide* link to the item. An item cannot be marked reviewed while its category is still Unsorted.
- **Captured items are saved as needing review.** A classified capture is also not offered to phones until someone signs it off, because Self-Service offers any reviewed, active consumable. The finish screen offers one button, *Mark N items reviewed*, for exactly the classified ones; Select mode and the item form do the same.
- **Saving survives a dropped connection** (`src/catalogue-outbox.ts`, IndexedDB on the device). Every capture is written there before any request, removed only when the server has the record and its photo, and shown with its state (*Saving…*, *Saved*, *Not saved yet*, *Saved, photo to send*, *Needs you*). The request id is the server's key, so a retry, or a save whose answer was lost, never makes a second item (`catalogue_captures.id`). A reload keeps unsent items and sends them; an ended sign-in stops the queue without hammering the server; an item held for a session that is now finished is listed on the Catalogue page with a Discard. This is not an offline mode: nothing is saved without the server, and V1.6 builds the offline queue on this same boundary.
- **Select mode in Items** (`src/bulk-select.ts`, `src/bulk.ts`): checkboxes only while selecting, shift-click for a range, select all shown. A bar says how many are selected; the dialog shows what will change (count, names, how many already have it, how many will be skipped and why) before its button, which names the count. Actions: move to a place, set the category, set the stock area, mark reviewed. Quantity is never touched. Sent in pieces of 50 with the version each item had on screen; an item someone else changed meanwhile is skipped and named; every changed item gets its own `ITEM_UPDATED` history entry marked as one of N edited together.
- **Quantity-based records.** An item is still one parent record whose quantity is its movements; the capture's count is its opening-balance movement. No asset-level tracking was added: the optional model and serial number are fields on the item (shown in the profile, editable in the item form, and used as duplicate signals).
- **Activity.** `CATALOGUE_STARTED` and `CATALOGUE_FINISHED` entries (place, counts) and "while cataloguing" on the item's creation entry; filterable under Catalog.

## Permission notes

Any signed-in Logistics staff member (role STAFF with DoL access, ADMIN, OWNER) can catalogue and use Select mode, as they can already edit items; staff of other departments and officers get 403 as for every Hub route. A session can be added to, moved and finished only by the person who started it; anyone with Hub access can read it. Captures never publish anything: lending audience starts as not lendable and the record starts as needing review. Select mode's *Mark reviewed* is the same act as ticking "reviewed" in the item form.

## Known limitations

- Real phones, real cameras and screen readers were not tested; the photo input asks the phone for its camera (`capture="environment"`) and the evidence chose a file instead.
- The photo-looks-the-same hint is a 64-bit hash: a re-shot object warns, two similar objects photographed alike will too, and two angles of one object will not.
- The queue holds unsent items on this device only; a different device does not see them, and they are lost if the browser's data is cleared before they are sent. Where the browser refuses storage (a private window), they live in the page and the screen says to stay on it.
- Offline cataloguing, installability, the physical-audit discrepancy session, kits and containers, Self-Service 2.0 and any AI classification are not in this slice (V1.6 onward).
- On a 390 px phone the save bar takes about a fifth of the screen when "Save as a separate item" wraps to three lines.
- Review later uses placeholders (category Unsorted, unit piece) until someone decides; they are hidden from the category pickers and the item form shows the category empty, but a report that groups by category would show "Unsorted".
- The Catalogue page lists the newest 100 review-later items and a session page the newest 40 captures; older ones are found under Items.
