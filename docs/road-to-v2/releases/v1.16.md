## What changed for staff since V1.15

- **Optional assistance (A1, A2, AI-P01).** Photo and name suggestions run in the background. They never block saving, stock or lending, never change stock, and step aside on a quota, outage or stale answer. A photo's suggested name only fills an empty name field for a person to check; a text suggestion that no reviewed catalogue entry uniquely supports is dropped; a person's typing always wins.
- **Grounded Home cards (B1).** Home's cards use only current, active items and link to their sources, the existing caption says how fresh they are, and an answer that arrives after someone leaves Home is ignored. Stock, the Catalogue and Search keep their exact, source-backed paths.
- **Reviewed learning (C1).** Staff corrections are recorded with their evidence. Catalogue knowledge changes only through a reviewed proposal, never by model self-training.
- **Version and what's new (D1).** Administration > System > This version shows the product version and its short notes, from the deploy's private build record.
- **Add items prefill (PR48).** A name that is exactly a reviewed item's name, or whose five look-alikes all agree, fills an empty Category and Counted in and says so underneath. Anything weaker stays a button, and a person's own entry always wins.
- **Look and feel (PR51, PR55, PR57).** Home leads with the greeting and the one thing to act on, with quick actions as tiles; an item's profile opens as a panel beside its row; an overdue loan shows a quiet edge rule instead of a pink box, and a loan with a due date gets a small time track; item photos sit on a plain stone backdrop; empty states are quieter; and Staff has a Dark theme from the account menu's sun and moon button.
- **Staff Directory links (QH02).** A Staff Directory link with an unknown filter shows Everyone instead of failing.
- **Faster staff pages (MA1–MA3).** Sign-in loads 102 KB of JavaScript instead of 243 KB, and most other staff pages about half as much as before; Items, now split into shared pieces, stays about the same. In first-visit timings on an emulated slow phone connection, sign-in went from 1,209 to 960 ms and Home from 1,568 to 1,230 ms, Items was 50 ms slower and Self-Service unchanged; other pages were not timed. The Staff Directory routes, the staff shell and the photo viewer live in their own modules behind the same central checks.

## Known limits, accepted with the owner's merge of this release

- Production deploys through Cloudflare Workers Builds, not through CI: a push to `main` goes live about a minute later, before CI finishes. QH03's single CI path is the separate V1.17 task.
- Gemma real-photo qualification is partial: 9 of 40 planned cases were attempted, and 5 of those 9 were named correctly. Printed identifiers, negative and fallback cases and the calculator case are unmeasured, so photo names stay suggestions a person confirms.
- Model precision after the parser and schema correction, session, optional-offer and original-photo counts, and production audit counts are unmeasured.
- The Add items prefill makes no production accuracy claim: its seed-catalogue figures say how often it is right when it fills a field, not how often it will fill one on a new shelf.
- No production evidence for: provider faults (403, 429, timeout, 5xx, invalid schema, stale answer), quota exhaustion, a missing AI binding or R2 bucket, parallel staff edits, stale app shells, Self-Service Test mode, Windows High Contrast and 400% zoom.
- "Approve for code review" needs three corrections from two staff and has not been exercised in production.
