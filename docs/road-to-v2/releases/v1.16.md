## What changed for staff since V1.15

- **Optional assistance (A1, A2, AI-P01).** Photo and name suggestions run in the background. They never block saving, stock or lending, never change stock, and step aside on a quota, outage or stale answer. A suggestion that no reviewed catalogue entry uniquely supports is dropped, and a person's typing always wins.
- **Grounded hints (B1).** Hints in Home, Attention, Stock and the Catalogue come only from verified, source-linked data; Search stays exact.
- **Reviewed learning (C1).** Staff corrections are recorded with their evidence. Catalogue knowledge changes only through a reviewed proposal, never by model self-training.
- **Version and what's new (D1).** Administration > System > This version shows the product version and its short notes, from the deploy's private build record.
- **Add items prefill (PR48).** A name that matches reviewed items fills Category and Counted in, and says so underneath.
- **Look and feel (PR51, PR55, PR57).** A staged Home, attached item profiles and a quieter Loans page; photos on a stone stage; quieter empty states; a Staff Dark theme from the account menu's sun and moon button.
- **Navigation and Directory (QH01, QH02).** Item cards open in a new tab with the browser's own modifier keys; a Staff Directory link with an unknown filter shows Everyone instead of failing.
- **Faster staff pages (MA1–MA3).** Sign-in loads 102 KB of JavaScript instead of 243 KB, and most staff pages about half as much as before. The Staff Directory routes, the staff shell and the photo viewer live in their own modules behind the same central checks.
