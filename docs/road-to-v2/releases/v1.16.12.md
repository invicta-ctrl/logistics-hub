## What changed

**One pager** (`src/pager.ts`, `src/pager-math.ts`, `src/styles.css`):
- `ListPager` gives a list numbered pages of 25 on desktop and "Show 25 more" below 48rem.
  - It writes `?page=` (or the list's own key) for a page's main list.
  - It starts again at page 1 when the question changes, and reveals a deep-linked row.
- `landOn` moves focus to the first row of a new page, or to the first new row.
- `foldMarkup` and `Folds` give a phone group a sticky title with its count, its first 5 rows and "Show all N".
  - `stickBelow` holds the titles under the page's own sticky bar.
  - `landOnFold` brings a closed fold's title back into view.
- `stepsMarkup` gives Newer and Older to a history read by cursor.
- The current page is filled oxblood. Every target is at least 2.5rem. Turning a page scrolls instantly under reduced motion.

**Where:**
- **Items** (`src/staff.ts`): the 100-row growth becomes pages.
- **Lending Hub** (`src/public.ts`): desktop pages are grouped by category within the page, and the headings count the whole group. On a phone, categories fold.
- **Self-Service browse** (`src/self-service-app.ts`): the same as the Lending Hub.
- **Stock** (`src/stock-workspace.ts`): attention, restock, pantry and activity.
- **Loans** (`src/loans-workspace.ts`): out and history.
- **Staff Directory** (`src/directory-workspace.ts`): pages grouped by department. On a phone, departments fold. A person's Usage tab replaces its 100-row growth with pages.
- **Kits** (`src/kits-workspace.ts`): kits and templates.
- **Administration** (`src/admin.ts`, `src/admin-catalog.ts`): staff accounts, knowledge proposals and other names.
- **Self-Service staff records** (`src/self-service-review.ts`): pages over the loaded records. "Load older records" still fetches the next batch.
- **Attention** (`src/attention-workspace.ts`): an opened group of more than 25 (the Worker sends up to 100 per reason) pages within the group.
- **Activity** (`src/activity-workspace.ts`):
  - Desktop shows Newer and Older, 25 at a time. Older past what is loaded reads the next cursor batch first.
  - The phone feed still loads as it scrolls, up to 300. Its rows out of sight skip layout and paint (`content-visibility: auto`; the focus ring is drawn inside the row, so it is not clipped).

**Not paged**, with reasons in spec §5: the Locations tree, stock checks, in-sheet histories with "Show older", closed Attention groups, Self-Service attention cards, the search palette and home summaries.
