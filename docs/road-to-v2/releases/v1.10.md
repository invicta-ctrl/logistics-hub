## What shipped

- **Attention domain and API** (`src/attention.ts`): `GET /api/staff/attention` (entries grouped by reason, at most 100 listed per reason, with true totals and totals by urgency) and `GET /api/staff/attention/summary` (counts only). Derived on request from existing records; **nothing is stored and there is no migration**. Each reason is one SQL `FROM … WHERE` used for both the list and the count, so they cannot disagree.
- **The bell and numbers in the staff bar** (`src/staff.ts`): a bell with the total beside the account, and numbers on Loans and Stock. One small request after a page is drawn, kept 30 seconds, refreshed every 60 seconds while the page is visible, and failing quietly. Counts cap at 99+, with text for screen readers.
- **The Attention page** (`/staff/attention`, `src/attention-workspace.ts`, `src/attention.css`): grouped by how soon (Today, This week, When there is time) and by reason; filters by source, how soon and age, kept in the address; five entries per group with **Show the first N**; the split "21 to act on · 394 when there is time" matching the bell; a calm empty state; **Mark reviewed** in place for damaged and lost returns; refreshes by itself.
- **Contextual resolution:** every entry links to where the next step is made. The Loans page focuses and marks the loan (`?loan=`, also for the return history), and an item's Details opens from `?item=…&tab=details`. An Unclassified item's Details shows the top suggestion with how sure it is (Suggested, Maybe, or both options when they conflict) and its reason, with **Use suggestion** filling the form without saving (amendment §11 item, Rules A–G).
- **Mark reviewed:** `POST /api/staff/loans/:id/review` writes one `LOAN_REVIEWED` audit entry per loan (idempotent) that the Activity feed shows as "X reviewed the damaged return of Y". It changes no stock and no loan.
- **Small supporting changes:** `UNSETTLED_FINDING` in `src/audits.ts` is now shared by an item's freshness and by Attention, so both say the same thing; `kitsNotReady` in `src/kits.ts`; the Self-Service number in the bar now comes from the same definitions as the inbox's Self-Service groups.
- **V1.9 loose end fixed:** closing a Self-Service form that was opened from a direct link left Self-Service (blank page). It now shows the item's page (`src/self-service-app.ts`, test in `tests/browser/self-service-v19.spec.ts`; commit `44abd4d`).

## Attention rules

Full table in `docs/PRODUCT_REFERENCE.md` ("Attention (V1.10)"). Urgency is one fixed rule per reason, never a score. Summary:

| Reason | Source | Clears when |
|---|---|---|
| Overdue loan (Today) | Loans | the loan is returned |
| Damaged or lost return (This week; last 60 days) | Loans | someone marks it reviewed, or after 60 days (it stays in the loan history) |
| Phone record to check (Today after a day, else This week) | Self-Service | staff resolve the record |
| Report from a phone (This week) | Self-Service | the report is resolved |
| Out of stock (Today with a reorder level, else This week; routine once a restock is requested) | Stock | stock is received, or the item becomes inactive |
| Low stock (This week; routine once a restock is requested) | Stock | stock goes above the level |
| Check finding to settle (This week) | Locations | a resolution, a later look at the item, or a later count |
| Reported more than once (This week) | Locations | reports are resolved so fewer than two are open |
| Kit to replenish (This week) / review (routine) | Kits | the kit is Ready |
| Record that looks wrong (routine) | Catalog | the item is edited after the check, or by the finding rules |
| Item to classify (routine) | Catalog | the item is classified |
| Item without a place (routine) | Catalog | a place is set |

## Performance evidence

Attention is computed per request, so its cost is its queries. `tests/attention-performance.test.ts` seeds 3,400 items, 65,000 movements, 5,000 loans, 800 audit observations, 600 reports and 4,000 Self-Service records, about three times the real hub, on the `node:sqlite` stand-in (query work, not the network round trip to D1; read the shape, not the absolute figure). Medians of 15 runs:

| What | Before the stock rules shared one pass | Final |
|---|---|---|
| Shell numbers (`/attention/summary`) | 84 ms | **47 ms** |
| Inbox (`/attention`, all reasons, 100 per reason) | 164 ms | **135 ms** |
| The session route's Self-Service count (on every staff page's path) | 0.17 ms (V1.9 query) | **0.72 ms** |

The numbers are fetched after a page is drawn, so they are off the navigation path. The measured navigation, rendered with the real application on a throwaway Worker (`timings-*.json`, median of seven): cold load to the first Items row 452 ms on V1.9 and 461 / 433 ms in two runs on V1.10; an Items → Stock switch 99 ms on V1.9 and 91 / 89 ms. No meaningful change. On a cold open the first Attention row draws in 0.23–0.36 s. Out and Low stock are counted in one pass over the movement ledger, which dominates the cost; the test asserts they equal the lists. No cache and no background job were needed. Real D1 and the network were not measured.

## Known limitations

- **Catalog photos are not a reason.** Photos are optional in the product, so a missing one is not "required"; the other completeness reasons (classification, place) are covered.
- **Offline sync conflicts are not a reason.** They exist only on the device that made them (V1.6), so the server cannot list them; the device already shows them in the Catalog.
- **A damaged or lost return older than 60 days** is no longer asked for; it stays in the loan history. The window is `RETURN_PROBLEM_DAYS` in `src/attention.ts`.
- **"Out of stock" follows the Stock page's rule**, so an active record with no movements (such as a migrated record never counted) is listed as out.
- **Lists are bounded to 100 per reason**, with the true total shown; a long routine list (141 Unclassified items in the demo) shows the first 100 by name.
- A group's count in the page is exact, but an entry can change between two refreshes (the page polls every 15 seconds).
- Real D1 latency, real phones, a screen reader and a Windows run were not tested.
