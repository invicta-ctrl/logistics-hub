## Insight definitions and time windows

All are read from existing records in one `db.batch` with no new table or index; each card shows its question, its window and its rule, and each row its evidence in a sentence. None is a score, none names a borrower or an account, nothing is ordered or changed, and a card with nothing that meets its rule is left out. Windows end at the request.

| Card | Source | Window | Rule | Advisory |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What equipment is borrowed most? | `loans` | 90 days | lent at least twice; most first, then latest | no |
| Which consumables go fastest? | posted `STOCK_OUT` movements of Consumables, imports excluded | 30 days | taken out at least twice; most units first | yes: shows what is left and the reorder level |
| What keeps running short? | `reorders`, not dismissed | 90 days | requested at least twice | yes |
| Where do people keep failing to find things? | `location_reports` by place | 90 days | places with at least two reports | no |
| Which kits keep needing a refill? | `kit_checks` with a Low or Missing observation (Damaged is a repair) | 90 days | at least two such checks, active kits | no |
| What keeps being reclassified? (amendment) | `ITEM_UPDATED` audit rows that changed behaviour, not the first sort out of "not sure" | 90 days | changed at least twice | no |
| How complete is the catalog? (amendment) | active items | today | Unclassified and without a place, each linking to Attention's reason | no |

The completeness gaps use the same SQL predicates as Attention's CLASSIFY and NO_PLACE reasons (`GAP_UNCLASSIFIED`, `GAP_NO_PLACE`, exported from `src/attention.ts`), so Home's number and the Attention list are the same set. At most five rows per card.

## Performance evidence

From `tests/home-performance.test.ts` (Node 22, 4-core cloud container, `node:sqlite` for D1; read the shape, not the absolute figure), 2026-10-07, on a catalogue of realistic size with a busy ledger:

| What | Median | Worst | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `resumable` (Continue) | 0.20 ms | 0.31 ms | two indexed lookups |
| `insights` (all cards, one batch) | 87.3 ms | 91.6 ms | cached for 2 minutes by the browser; loaded after the page |
| the Attention summary (shared with the bell) | 50.0 ms | | Home adds no request for it |

The test prints each insight's query plan and fails if the usage insight reads the whole ledger or `resumable` slows. The usage insight first ran at 82.5 ms by walking the item index through every movement; pinning it to the date index (`INDEXED BY idx_inventory_movements_created`) bounds it to its 30-day window and removed the scan (the two runs' totals differ by noise, the plan is what changed). Reports and the corrections card use a temp B-tree for `GROUP BY` over bounded windows. No index or table was added.

Rendered on a throwaway Worker (`docs/visual-research/v1.12/after/timings.json`, one run per size): Needs attention on screen 113–171 ms after navigation and insights 133–192 ms; a cold load to the first Items row 301 ms and an Items → Stock switch 67 ms, unchanged from V1.11. Real D1 and the network were not measured.

## Decisions

- **Home is not a seventh section.** The bar keeps its six; the logo, the account menu, search and sign-in lead to Home.
- **Sign-in lands on Home** (was Items). `/staff/admin` still sends staff to Items.
- **No browser-stored personalization.** Role decides the Quick actions; recent explicit workflow shows up as Continue (server-side, only when the work exists).
- **No cached summary table.** The bounded insights run in one batch after the page is drawn and the browser keeps them for 2 minutes; measured above.

## Known limitations

- **Home shows at most six Attention reasons.** The rest are behind **Everything in Attention (N more)**.
- **Insights are only as useful as the history behind them.** A card is hidden until something repeats; a quiet hub shows "Nothing is repeating enough to show." beside the catalog's completeness.
- **The consumables card counts STOCK_OUT only** (not takes recorded as loans, and not imported history), and the reorder level shown is the one set on the item today.
- **The corrections card cannot tell why a use was changed**, only that it was changed more than once.
- **Insights are cached by the browser for 2 minutes**, so a change may take that long to show on a returning visit.
- Real D1 latency, real phones, a screen reader and a Windows run were not tested.
