## What changed

**Server** (`src/accounts.ts`):
- The access groups gain `DOL_DIRECTOR`.
- `hubAccess` admits `HUB_GROUPS`, which is DoL and DOL_DIRECTOR. Before, it admitted DoL alone.
- Nothing else reads the group to grant access. The Worker's one gate (`worker.ts`, `NO_HUB_ACCESS`) uses `hubAccess`.
- A Director is a `STAFF` account, so these rules treat them as they treat DoL Staff:
  - who may manage whom (`canManage`);
  - what an administrator may assign;
  - the session revocation on a role change.

**Client:**
- `accessLabel` reads "DoL Director".
- The role menu (`accessChoices`) offers it right after DoL Staff.
- "What each role can do" adds "The same as DoL Staff, for now."
- The role hint names the Director, and the Director's tag uses DoL Staff's green.
- The role-change confirmation counts the Director as a Logistics Hub user.
