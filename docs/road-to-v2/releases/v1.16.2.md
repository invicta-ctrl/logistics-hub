## What was wrong

Earl reported on 2026-10-24, with screenshots of Items in production, that removing a photo's background broke the list.
- **Cause.** A cut-out's list thumbnail is framed with the pedestal style. That rule set `position: relative` on the frame and outranked the list thumbnail's own `position: absolute` (`.item-thumb` in Items, `.thumb` in other staff tables).
- **Effect.** The thumbnail dropped out of its column onto the item's name, and the row grew. ITM-0463 and ITM-0464 showed it in production.
- **Second problem.** A photo taken against white got a white frame, which vanished into the row and left a small item floating (ITM-0473).

## What changed

- The pedestal asks for a positioned frame only at zero specificity (`:where`), so every thumbnail's own placement wins. The plinth still draws inside the frame.
- A list thumbnail whose frame takes the photo's colour keeps a 1px edge in the line colour.
- Both are CSS in `src/item-visual.css`. Nothing else changes.
