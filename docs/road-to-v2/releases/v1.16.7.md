## What changed

### Slice 1: item and place sheets

**The item sheet**
- The stage at the top of the sheet holds only the item's picture, at 16:10 on every screen.
  - A photo stands whole in the stage (contained, never cropped), and the same picture, blurred, fills the stage around it. The blurred copy is the same file as the photo, so it costs no request; nothing on the sheet asks for a display-size picture.
  - If the photo cannot load, the stage shows the item's icon, as before, and the blurred copy goes with it.
  - Without a photo, the item's icon stands centred at stage size, with Add photo under it. Pressing it opens the same menu as before: Add photo, or choose the icon. With large text the stage grows taller than 16:10 so the icon and the words both fit; a place without a picture does the same.
  - A cleaned photo keeps its pedestal, as a square scene centred in the stage.
  - The ID and name stay in the sheet's header.
- Overview is the item's profile, under the count (the + / − stepper stays first, and Lend stays the next step where it applies):
  - its next step, status and listing tags, type and category, recorded place with Where is it?, how fresh that is, and its kits;
  - Status, Lending Hub, Unit, Model, Serial number and Other names, in the facts list;
  - the review checklist, reports, availability and record, as before.
- Edit details holds Stock settings (reorder level, stock area, expiry, status, Add to restock, Add to kit) in their own group after the item's fields, with their own Save. The sticky Save bar of the item's fields never covers the group's heading.
- An inactive item's Loan tab links to Status in Stock settings, under Edit details, and puts the focus on it. Stock's hints about the reorder level and the pantry say Stock settings are under Edit details.

**Low & out of stock**
- Every row shows the item's list thumbnail, or its icon. It is All items' own thumbnail (same size and address, named by the item's last change), so a picture already seen in All items is not fetched again, and it loads only when the row is near the screen. The restock list shows it too.

**The place sheet (Locations)**
- A place opens on the item sheet's layout: a stage with the place's picture, then Overview, Edit details and History.
  - Overview: the place's path, its last check, any open reports, Check this place, and the items kept here (up to 12, each with its thumbnail, ID and count; with more, a link to all of them in Items).
  - Edit details: name, inside, directions, who sees them, In use, Move items and Delete.
  - History: the newest 15 entries of Activity for this place and the places inside it, each linked to its item, with a link to all of them in Activity.
- An item opened from a place is the item sheet of All items, with the same tabs and actions.
- A place just added opens on Edit details, where its directions go next; a place opened from the list or by its address opens on Overview. Saving, moving items or a picture change keeps the tab you were on.

**Interpretation recorded.** §3 lists "picture actions" under the place's Edit details. The picture's controls stay with the picture under its stage on every tab, as an item's photo controls do. A picture staged from the stage on Overview would otherwise have its Save in a hidden tab.

### Slice 2: loans, Self-Service and Activity

**Loans**
- A loan with a release photo shows a small thumbnail at the start of its row.
  - The staff browser draws the thumbnail once from the photo and saves it beside the photo. It is never sent to an image service.
  - It loads only when the row is near the screen, and only for staff.
- Every row opens the loan's sheet. The sheet shows:
  - the borrower: name, Student ID, use and reason;
  - the item, linked to its sheet;
  - quantity and dates;
  - who lent it and who closed it;
  - the photo, large, or a note that it was removed;
  - notes;
  - the loan's own trail from Activity.
- Return is offered in the sheet while the loan is out.

**Self-Service (staff page)**
- One Overview: what needs attention first, then the last 7 days of records, newest first.
- "Show all records" loads older records page by page, with Activity's paging. Search covers whatever is shown. The QR code and poster tab stays.
- The old addresses `?view=attention` and `?view=activity` land on Overview.

**Activity**
- Each entry is one line saying what happened, to what, by whom and when.
- An entry opens in place to its full detail: before and after values, quantities, place, reason, links to the item and the loan, and the request reference.
  - Only one entry is open at a time.
  - Entries open from the keyboard.
- "All activity for this item" leads to Activity filtered to that item, and Self-Service's activity links lead to Activity filtered to Self-Service.
- The new `loan` filter shows one loan's trail.

### Durable loan thumbnails (2026-11-03 amendment)

- A thumbnail is written only by create-if-absent (`If-None-Match: *`). A later save never replaces it.
- When owner retention erases a photo, it first records the key in the D1 journal `retention_photo_cleanup` (migration 0031). It then replaces the thumbnail with a permanent zero-byte, non-image erasure marker, and only then deletes the original.
  - Retries and concurrent runs share the same journal row.
  - The marker is never deleted. Earl confirmed on 2026-11-04 that the evidence bucket has no lifecycle rule that deletes objects.
- Thumbnails are served `private, no-store`. The list asks for `/thumb?v=2`, so a 24-hour copy cached by an earlier build is never reused.

### Slice 3: Administration and the rail

**Administration**
- System, Self-Service, Catalog, Staff and Accountability share one shape:
  - a verdict;
  - what needs a decision;
  - a status summary;
  - the settings in titled groups.
- Every change opens the same confirm-impact sheet: Now → After, who it affects, and how to undo it.
  - The sheet closes only when the Worker accepts the change.
  - A refusal stays in the sheet.
- On a phone the section tabs are one bar with a sliding marker. A move between sections never redraws the title.
- With large text the settings stack, so the description column stays readable.

**Rail**
- Each destination is its own raised plate. The current one is lit, and the lit plate travels to the next destination on a page move.
- One chevron control folds Items' six children and the Circulation, Records and Manage groups.
  - The control is a button with `aria-expanded`.
  - The choice is remembered on this device.
  - Opening a page unfolds its group.
  - Folded, Items keeps its Low & out of stock count.
- Pages settle in with a short fade and lift. Sheets slide in on a spring curve.
- A slow glint crosses the lit plate, and live status dots breathe.
  - Both are paused while the tab is hidden (`is-hidden-tab`, set in `src/staff-shell.ts`).
  - Neither runs with reduced motion.

**Deviations recorded**
- **Page moves:** they use the Web Animations API on the new page, not the View Transitions API. `startViewTransition` updates the page asynchronously, but callers read the page synchronously right after `shell()`. So the old page has no fade-out.
- **Rail folds:** they animate `grid-template-rows`, a layout property, on the small rail. Everything else moves by transform and opacity.
- **Verdict edge:** the verdict's 4 px coloured left edge stays as the accepted direction's coloured rule. The design pass that was to settle it was not run (see Verification).

## Rollback

Rollback is not yet approved or proven. V1.16.6 retention does not understand V1.16.7 thumbnails or the cleanup journal and can orphan thumbnails; a compatible forward fix or a retention fence plus reconciliation is required. Restoring D1 does not reverse R2 erasure markers. Verify backup, recovery and rollout compatibility through Cloud Operations before release.

Observed 2026-11-03 (source read, not production): retention has no Worker cron or scheduled trigger; it runs only when an owner presses it in Administration (`POST /api/staff/admin/retention`). V1.16.6 `812013f` has no loan-thumbnail code. A fence is therefore possible as procedure: no owner retention run while V1.16.6 serves after a rollback, then a reconciliation that overwrites each `loans/*.thumb` whose original is gone with the erasure marker.

Proposed rollback procedure, 2026-11-04 (written, not yet approved by Earl or rehearsed):
1. Roll back the Worker only. Leave migration 0031 applied: its table and triggers are additive, and V1.16.6 neither reads nor conflicts with them (a trigger refuses only reuse of a photo key already queued for erasure, which V1.16.6 never does, because loan keys are `loans/<loan id>`).
2. While V1.16.6 serves, the owner does not press Remove in Administration > Accountability. In V1.16.6, retention is the only path that deletes a loan photo another record still uses. Its other delete (`dropUnusedPhoto`) only removes an upload no record uses, which never has a thumbnail. So with no retention run, no thumbnail can be orphaned.
3. Roll forward to V1.16.7 (or later). Retention then drains any PENDING journal rows and continues normally.
4. If retention was run on V1.16.6 by mistake, any thumbnail of an erased photo stays as a private JPEG that no route serves (V1.16.6 has no thumbnail route; V1.16.7 serves only thumbnails of photos a record still holds). A sweep is then needed: list `*.thumb` keys in the evidence bucket and overwrite each whose original key no record references with the erasure marker. Earl decided on 2026-11-04 ("Procedure only"): no sweep is built. Step 2 is the control, and the residual risk of a mistaken run is accepted and recorded here.

Restoring D1 from a backup taken before V1.16.7 does not remove R2 erasure markers. They hold zero image bytes and stay harmless under V1.16.6.
