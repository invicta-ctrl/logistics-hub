## What changed

**Ambient AI Assist.** This is the 2026-10-08 amendment. It has no staff-facing AI surface: no tab, no chat, no prompt box, no percentages and no model choice.
- **Photo name:** a Catalog photo, taken online, may suggest the item's name. It goes to `@cf/google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it` with thinking off. The hint reads "Suggested from the photo. Check it, or type over it."
- **Possible duplicate:** after a photo syncs, one check may raise a "Possible duplicate" Attention reason. "Keep both" settles it and is audited.
- **AI never writes:** it cannot change an item, stock, a loan or any other authoritative state.
- **Neuron bands per UTC day:**
  - 0 to 6,500: normal;
  - 6,500 to 8,000: conserve;
  - 8,000 to 9,000: borrow the reserve;
  - 9,000 to 9,500: critical, where only a person waiting may call;
  - 9,500 and above: stop.
- **Spending is counted first:** the reserve is taken in one conditional write before the call, so two calls near a line cannot both start, and corrected afterwards.
- **A check that could not run waits:** a duplicate check skipped by the switch, the budget, the breaker or a provider failure is kept and retried on a later photo sync, at most two per sync.
- **Possible existing item:** each match offers Open and **Use existing**, which opens the item already in the catalog and drops the capture. "Save as a separate item" is Keep as new.
- **Breaker:** each call times out after 10 s, and repeated failures pause the AI for 10 minutes.
- **Owner switch:** Administration > System, audited.
- **Not built, and why:** Qwen, GLM, Granite at runtime and AI Gateway are listed in the plan under "Not built". The deferred V1.11 measurement was negative, so no model-chosen category or unit ships.
- **What reaches AI:** only the intentional catalog photo. Never Student IDs, staff ID scans, faces, Self-Service proof, evidence or notes. `tests/catalog-ai.test.ts` asserts the boundary.

**Mobile-first and perceived performance:**
- Every staff page shows its bar and shape at once instead of a blank screen.
- Layout shift is 0.10 or less on every route on a held connection. It was 0.25 on Items.
- Nothing scrolls sideways at any of six widths. The 320 px bar and the strip clipping are fixed.
- Saves are acknowledged at once and say "done" only after the commit.
- On a touch phone, controls reach 44 px, with safe-area insets and sheets that fit at 320 px with 200% text.
- A real-Worker sweep uses every page's own controls at 390 and 1440 px.

**Identity, semantics and Activity:**
- Take, Use, Borrow and Return need full name, an 8-digit Student ID and a photo. The Worker refuses anything else.
- A Return starts as the borrower, and "Not you? Change" records whoever hands it back instead.
- The "Identity needs review" Attention reason has Confirm and Ignore, both audited.
- Staff Directory usage links by exact Student ID only and includes phone Uses, paged by 100.
- There is one tab and segmented-control style.
- The wording sweep found no enum names, no Title Case and no "verified" claim.
- The cataloguing page is now **Add items**, and public copy writes **DoL staff**. Both are Earl's defaults.
- Activity is a full-height workspace.
- The ID card's scans load in order, time out and offer Try again.

**Storage:**
- Self-Service proof photos are 1280 px at quality 0.75, about half the bytes (Earl's default).
- Modelled on V1.14's bands, at 20 Takes and Uses a day: about 1.5 GB a year, or 15% of R2's free 10 GB. That is green.

## Privacy

- **Service worker:** the caches still hold only the shell and the brand and QR files. `/api/` is never intercepted, so no record, photo, scan or Student ID enters a cache. The only change to `src/sw.ts` is the origin check.
- **Staff ID scans:** they stay in their own bucket, behind staff-only routes, with a 20 s timeout.
- **Workers AI** receives the one catalog photo the staff member took for that item. Nothing is stored at the provider by the Hub, and no per-item output was committed.
- **`npm run verify:privacy`:** 0 secret matches and 0 roster matches.
- **Screenshots:** all of them use fixture data.

## Migrations and data

- No migration.
- New `system_settings` keys:
  - `ambient_assist`, the switch (absent means on);
  - `ai_neurons:<UTC date>`;
  - `assist_photo_match:<itemId>`;
  - `assist_recheck:<itemId>`, a duplicate check waiting to run, cleared once it runs;
  - `identity_rule_from`, written with the first phone record saved under the identity rule, so Identity needs review only looks at records from then on.
- New Attention reasons: `POSSIBLE_DUPLICATE` and the Identity needs review reason. Both are derived, and their decisions are audited.
- Old loans and phone records keep whatever Student ID text they had. Only new records must carry 8 digits.
