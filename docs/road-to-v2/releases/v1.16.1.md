## What changed for staff and visitors

- **Front page (S02).** An oxblood band with the HAU USC crest and a "Find equipment" search that opens the Lending Hub with the words typed. It makes no catalog request of its own. Under it, a strip of the Logistics services and the borrowing steps.
- **Staff navigation (S01).**
  - On computers: a grouped oxblood rail (Resources, Circulation, Records, Manage), with Find, the attention bell, the department and the account.
  - On tablets: a 72 px icon rail with tooltips and an All destinations menu.
  - On phones: Home, Find and More.
- **Home and Attention are one workspace (S01).**
  - One queue, with reason buttons that filter it in place.
  - The chosen entry's record opens beside the queue, or in a sheet on smaller screens. Back closes it.
  - Continue and Insights sit beside the queue. Home opens on today and this week; Attention opens on everything.
- **Catalog and checks (S03, decisions D1, D2, D2a and D3).**
  - On the website, administrators catalogue in Administration › Catalog, in a sheet. The place is part of the sheet, the sheet stays open after each save, and Finish ends the session there.
  - Staff catalogue in the installed Catalog app. In a browser tab, `/staff/catalogue` shows only how to install it and the way back.
  - Place checks start from a place's "Check this place" in Locations.
- **Pictures (S03).**
  - A staff picture taken against a plain background is framed in that colour.
  - A picture whose background was removed stands on a pedestal.
  - Clicking an item photo opens one menu with all of its controls: view larger, change, take, crop the thumbnail, remove or restore its background, and remove (owner direction, 24 October 2026).
