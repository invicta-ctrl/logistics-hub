## What changed

**Tokens and shape** (`src/styles.css`):
- Radii are 8 (controls), 12 (cards and panels) and 16 (sheets and dialogs) (D6). `--dur-1` is 150 ms and the other durations are 200 ms.
- The gold mark on light surfaces is `#a87a12` (3.84:1 on white; D5) and the gold fill is `#E4B84F` with dark text or on maroon. The focus ring is opaque gold (it was 1.5:1). Gold is never text on a light surface.
- The hairline `--line` is slightly firmer so a tile's edge reads on the paper.
- No bevel, glow or gradient: the primary button, the public hero, the rail (plates and lit marker), the photo stage (halo, wash and lifted shadow), the plate, the overdue loan row, the skeletons.
- No coloured edge stripe on a table row, an overdue loan or a dialog.
- Pressed colours: `--press-brand` and `--press-tint` (light and dark), at least 4.5:1 under the label.

**Components:**
- Solid maroon primary, outlined maroon secondary, solid chosen segment. A pressed button darkens; hover on the secondary stays as quiet as before; Self-Service keeps its own colours.
- Status tags that carry a tone are tinted pills with a glyph and words. They wrap inside their own box at large text and stay one line in a table, where they remain the compact dotted label until the Inventory slice (S2).
- Skeletons are still blocks; empty-error states are a tinted panel; long words wrap.
- A pressed, waiting button shows a spinner (the one thing that turns, only while work is under way). The spinner takes its room from the button's padding and the width it had is kept as a minimum, so the button and its neighbours do not move. A message or a clear button arrives with a short motion; press feedback on chips, segments and pager.
- Search fields are pills; phone fields are 48 px; touch targets reach 44 px with an invisible hit slop on the clear buttons.
- The record ID in a sheet header is plain mono text, not a decorative tag.
- A toast respects a side safe-area inset in both its position and its width.
- Forced colours: the status glyph and the chosen segment stay visible.

**The public home page (`/`, authorized by Earl on 2026-11-06, see the top):**
- A slow water (WebGL caustics) behind the heading, a fine grain over the page, and the campus photograph above the footer. The water is lazy-loaded (`import("./water")`) and decorative only: the page is complete without it, and `/brand/water-still.webp` (14,506 bytes) is the picture the layer shows whenever the water is stopped.
- It never starts under reduced motion, Data Saver or forced colours (and stops if they are switched on while the page is open); it pauses while the tab is hidden or the heading is scrolled away; it runs at 30 fps on touch screens, caps its drawing size, falls back to the still image if WebGL is unavailable or lost, and removes every listener and its GPU context when the router leaves the page. It makes no network call and keeps no pointer data.
- The grain and the water take no clicks and are hidden from assistive technology. The header and footer respect the safe-area insets. The hero eyebrow is the light text colour (the gold failed contrast over the moving water).
- Not taken from the supplied zip, deliberately: its global `:root`, reset and grain rules, its global reduced-motion rule and the squircle corner rule. They would have changed every other screen or clashed with S1's matched corners.

**Breakpoint and polish:**
- The staff app, its lists and the shared stylesheet share one 760 px phone breakpoint (`src/pager.ts`, the filter bar and the activity feed). It was 47.99 rem for lists. Self-Service keeps its own 48 rem layout switch in `src/self-service.css`; it belongs to S4 and is not claimed as part of this breakpoint.
- Tabular numbers, `text-wrap: pretty`, safe-area insets on sheet headers and toasts, continuous corners where the browser supports `corner-shape`.
- Looping removed: the skeleton shimmer and the hero rise are gone; the Self-Service skeleton is flat.
