## What changed

**Tokens and shape** (`src/styles.css`):
- Radii are 8 (controls), 12 (cards and panels) and 16 (sheets and dialogs) (D6). `--dur-1` is 150 ms and the other durations are 200 ms.
- The gold mark on light surfaces is `#a87a12` (3.84:1 on white; D5) and the gold fill is `#E4B84F` with dark text or on maroon. The focus ring is opaque gold (it was 1.5:1). Gold is never text on a light surface.
- The hairline `--line` is slightly firmer so a tile's edge reads on the paper.
- No bevel, glow or gradient: the primary button, the public hero, the rail (plates and lit marker), the photo stage (halo, wash and lifted shadow), the plate, the overdue loan row, the skeletons.
- No coloured edge stripe on a table row, an overdue loan or a dialog.

**Components:**
- Solid maroon primary, outlined maroon secondary, solid chosen segment.
- Status tags that carry a tone are tinted pills with a glyph and words. In a table row they stay the compact dotted label until the Inventory slice (S2).
- Skeletons are still blocks; empty-error states are a tinted panel; long words wrap.
- A pressed, waiting button shows a spinner (the one thing that turns, only while work is under way); a message or a clear button arrives with a short motion; press feedback on chips, segments and pager.
- Search fields are pills; phone fields are 48 px; touch targets reach 44 px with an invisible hit slop on the clear buttons.
- The record ID in a sheet header is plain mono text, not a decorative tag.

**Review R1 (independent, on the exact diff):** four defects were found and fixed in the source commit, with a regression test for the first two (`tests/browser/tab-style.spec.ts`): the touch hit-slop rule had set `position: relative` on the search clear and password reveal buttons and unseated them from their field; a gold tag (Officer, Admin, Planned) looked like a warning pill; the hero's focus ring was 2.8:1 on the maroon band; and a top safe-area pad on the bar left sticky offsets short on a notched device (removed).

**Breakpoint and polish:**
- One 760 px phone breakpoint for layout and for lists (`src/pager.ts`, the filter bar and the activity feed). It was 47.99 rem for lists.
- Tabular numbers, `text-wrap: pretty`, safe-area insets on sheet headers and toasts, continuous corners where the browser supports `corner-shape`.
- Looping removed: the skeleton shimmer and the hero rise are gone; the Self-Service skeleton is flat.

## Tests changed deliberately

- `tests/browser/tab-style.spec.ts`: the chosen segment is a solid maroon fill with no ring; the focus ring is the new gold.
- `tests/browser/v1167-sheets.spec.ts`: the photo stage's blurred halo is gone, so the ambient copy is `display: none`.

## Left for later slices (not S1)

- The iridescent public backdrop, hero and sign-in (S4). Bento tiles, steps and the other layout patterns arrive with their first real consumer.
