## What changed

**Tokens and shape** (`src/styles.css`):
- Radii are 8 (controls), 12 (cards and panels) and 16 (sheets and dialogs) (D6). `--dur-1` is 150 ms and the other durations are 200 ms.
- The gold mark on light surfaces is `#a87a12` (3.84:1 on white; D5) and the gold fill is `#E4B84F` with dark text or on maroon. The focus ring is opaque gold (it was 1.5:1). Gold is never text on a light surface.
- The hairline `--line` is slightly firmer so a tile's edge reads on the paper.
- No bevel, glow or gradient: the primary button, the public hero, the rail (plates and lit marker), the photo stage (halo, wash and lifted shadow), the plate, the overdue loan row, the skeletons.
- No coloured edge stripe on a table row, an overdue loan or a dialog.
- Pressed colours: `--press-brand` and `--press-tint` (light and dark), at least 4.5:1 under the label.

**Components:**
- Solid maroon primary, outlined maroon secondary, solid chosen segment. A pressed button darkens; hover on the secondary stays as quiet as before; Self-Service keeps its own colours.
- Status tags that carry a tone are tinted pills with a glyph and words. They wrap inside their own box at large text and stay one line in a table, where they remain the compact dotted label until the Inventory slice (S2).
- Skeletons are still blocks; empty-error states are a tinted panel; long words wrap.
- A pressed, waiting button shows a spinner (the one thing that turns, only while work is under way). The spinner takes its room from the button's padding and the width it had is kept as a minimum, so the button and its neighbours do not move. A message or a clear button arrives with a short motion; press feedback on chips, segments and pager.
- Search fields are pills; phone fields are 48 px; touch targets reach 44 px with an invisible hit slop on the clear buttons.
- The record ID in a sheet header is plain mono text, not a decorative tag.
- A toast respects a side safe-area inset in both its position and its width.
- Forced colours: the status glyph and the chosen segment stay visible.

**Breakpoint and polish:**
- The staff app, its lists and the shared stylesheet share one 760 px phone breakpoint (`src/pager.ts`, the filter bar and the activity feed). It was 47.99 rem for lists. Self-Service keeps its own 48 rem layout switch in `src/self-service.css`; it belongs to S4 and is not claimed as part of this breakpoint.
- Tabular numbers, `text-wrap: pretty`, safe-area insets on sheet headers and toasts, continuous corners where the browser supports `corner-shape`.
- Looping removed: the skeleton shimmer and the hero rise are gone; the Self-Service skeleton is flat.

## Visual and interaction evidence (at `4eae8dd`, spot re-measured after R4)

Run against a synthetic 580-item dataset (16 categories, 23 units, 25 locations, 10 kits, 30 loans with 6 overdue, 46 long names, 12 names with an unbroken 30+ character word), served twice with the identical dataset (sha256 `a5dbe88e9a2d5bc5b02d4ed9c3ad214e6d9083275643ab7e96354b59169d8a5e`): base `35f230f` and S1. Chromium only (Playwright); no WebKit, no real phone, no screen reader.

- **Width and theme matrix:** 8 widths (320, 375, 390, 430, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440) × light and staff-dark × reduced motion on and off × 19 pages = 608 captures per server; 344 state captures (loading, empty list, empty search, error 500, offline) and 208 component captures. S1 had zero horizontal overflow, zero clipped labels or concealed actions, zero gradients and edge stripes on staff pages, zero nested cards and zero non-tabular figures; every flag it raised was also raised on base, except the items fixed in R4. 304 reduced-motion pairs showed no difference.
- **200% text (WCAG 1.4.4, reported separately from page zoom):** the repository's own emulation (`html` font-size 200% `!important`), proven to scale text (16 → 32 px on all 640 captures; 97.2% of 76,325 text elements doubled). 22 page states × 8 widths × light and dark. Result: S1 153 FAIL / 166 PASS / 1 known limit of 320 cells; base 151 / 168 / 1. **This is not a pass, and it is mostly not S1's:** the failures are pre-existing (Items rows overlap, Loans loses its Return buttons off-screen at 320–768 and 1280, the Activity heading collapses, Public Lending overflows, definition lists clip). The two cells that were S1-only (Kits at 430) are fixed in R4; after R4 Kits at 320–375 is narrower than base (382 against 408 px) and fits at 390 and 430, and Locations at 320 equals base. Remaining text-scaling failures are deferred, below.
- **Full-page zoom (separate pass, 640 and 720 CSS px at device scale 2):** 80 of 80 captures PASS on both S1 and base.
- **Keyboard open (viewport shrunk to 390×390), focus, names, history, touch, safe areas, saving button:** results are in the packet; none is an S1 regression except the three fixed in R4/R5 and one informational note (a scroll-linked `strip-edges` animation appears in `getAnimations()` on strip pages; it moves nothing without scrolling).
- **`ui-media-theme.spec.ts`:** no conflict; its assertions re-measured on S1 match base.

## Found, not fixed (pre-existing; not S1; deferred, none blocks this slice)

Found by the S1 QA and also present on base `35f230f`:
- Items rows overlap or print over the quantity at 320–390 px for names with an unbroken 30+ character word, and at 200% text.
- Loans (320–768 and 1280 at 200% text): 25 Return buttons are cut off and unreachable; Activity heading collapses at 320–430 at 200%; Public Lending overflows at 320–430 at 200%.
- Error panels on seven staff pages offer no manual retry (only "Retrying automatically."), and the message lacks a full stop.
- Browser Back from an open item sheet leaves the page (the sheet adds no history entry) and loses a grown or scrolled list.
- The sticky Save bar in the item edit sheet can cover a field focused near the bottom (WCAG 2.4.11).
- No top safe-area padding on the staff app bar or public header; several controls below 44 px on phones; 25 buttons named only "Return" or "Stock in".
- `blur > 8 px` elevation shadows remain on sheets, dialogs, toasts and the sign-in panel (soft elevation, not glow or bevel); the public Self-Service hero has a text shadow (S4).

These belong to S2 (Inventory), S3 (Operations), S4 (Public and Self-Service) or a separate accessibility change. S2–S5 are not started; Issue #88 stays open.
