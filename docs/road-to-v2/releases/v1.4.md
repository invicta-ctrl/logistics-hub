## What shipped

- **Places.** Reusable locations in a hierarchy of at most five levels (Office → Storage Area → Cabinet 1 → Shelf 2 → Box), each with a name, an optional parent, plain-language directions, a sharing setting (staff only or shown in Self-Service), at most one reference picture, an active flag and audit metadata. Any signed-in Logistics staff member creates, edits, moves and deactivates them (they already edited location text); every change is an Activity entry. A place is never deleted: deactivating keeps every item's history, refuses while active places are inside it, and no item can be put in an inactive place (database triggers).
- **Items point at a place.** `items.location_id` replaces the free-text field in the item form with a picker (options carry the full path, "No place set", and a quick-add for a new place beside the one just chosen). The item's history records the place in words as it was then ("Office › Cabinet 1"). The typed text in `items.storage_location` is kept as migration evidence and is no longer read or written.
- **Reconciliation of what was typed (migration `0024`).** One place per distinct trimmed typed value, items linked by that exact value, **nothing merged**: "Cabinet 1" and "cabinet 1" become two places and the Locations page lists such look-alikes with how to combine them on purpose (*Move items*, which writes each item's history). A place starts shared with Self-Service only if an item in it was already offered to phones (the only way its text was public before); everything else starts staff-only. A blank value gets no place; an item whose typed location has no place shows "Needs a place" with the typed text. One `LOCATIONS_RECONCILED` audit entry stores the counts. No quantity, typed value or edit version changes.
- **One picture, many items.** A place's picture is stored once (`CATALOG_MEDIA`, `locations/<id>/display|thumb`) and shown for every item kept there, or inside it when a shelf has none of its own (the nearest picture on the route, captioned with the place it belongs to). Same safe JPEG path as item photos.
- **Where is it?** From the item profile (staff) and the item sheet (Self-Service): the route as steps with each step's directions, the picture (enlarge with the existing viewer), a "No picture yet" state, and the two reports. Staff-only places never reach a phone: the catalog carries a place only if it and every place above it is active and shared, and the picture addresses answer 404 otherwise.
- **I can’t find it / Location looks wrong.** Two non-destructive attention signals from staff (with an optional note) or from a phone (no free text; online-only; one open report per kind, item and network; rate-limited; nothing recorded from the Administration test panel). A report is an append-only record plus an audit entry; it never changes stock or an item's place. Staff see it on the item, in an Items view ("Location reports"), on the place, and in History; one person resolves it once, with a note.
- **Browsing.** Items filters by place (including everything inside it) and searches by path; Activity filters by a place's name or path; the Stock page shows each item's place path.
- **Lane.** Two optional manifest checks were added to the production-release script (`expect.derived`: counts taken before the migration must equal what it made; `expect.schemaChanged`: a column added to an existing table), with tests including a full fake-Cloudflare run.

## Permission notes

Any signed-in Logistics staff member (role STAFF with DoL access, ADMIN, OWNER) manages places and reports; staff of other departments and officers get 403, as for every Hub route. Self-Service sees a place only through the sharing rule above; reports from a phone carry no identity (a keyed network hash, as Self-Service records already do) and no free text.

## Known limitations

- Phone reports need a connection (the route itself works from the phone's last snapshot; the picture needs a connection). Offline reports and Self-Service 2.0 are later slices.
- A phone's open item sheet shows the catalog as it was when it opened (as its quantity line already did); reopening shows a changed place.
- A picture is public (both sizes) once its place is shared, by design, and only while Self-Service is open.
- Real phones and screen readers were not tested; pictures in the evidence are drawn, not photographs.
- Between applying the migrations and the deploy, an edit by the old code leaves an item "Needs a place" rather than linked; nothing is lost.
- The Locations page lists at most 500 places (a fixed bound so loading them stays a small read).
