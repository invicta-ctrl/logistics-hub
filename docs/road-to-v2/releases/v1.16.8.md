## What changed

### A return whose loan was already closed (bug)

Earl confirmed a Self-Service return held for checking, and it stayed in the list, saying "That loan was already closed".
- **Cause:** the return's own loan had already been closed in Loans. The Worker refused every confirm with 409, and the page did not refresh, so the record could never leave the list.
- **Fix in the Worker** (`resolveReview()`): when the return's own loan is already closed, confirming it clears the record. Stock was settled when the loan was closed, so nothing is applied again. It answers `{ resolved: true, alreadyClosed: true }`.
- **Still refused:**
  - a different closed loan;
  - a loan closed while staff were deciding (the race). These still answer 409, and the list now refreshes itself after a refusal.
- **The card:** when its loan is no longer open, it says the loan was already closed in Loans, so stock is already up to date, and offers Mark checked. After confirming, the toast says stock was not changed again.

### Borrowing from the Lending Hub (LH-LEND-01)

- Every row is one button (`aria-haspopup="dialog"`, described by its availability) that opens the item's sheet:
  - its picture;
  - how many are available;
  - what it is ("Borrow it and bring it back." or "Taken and not returned."), and "For USC use only." where that applies;
  - how to borrow it: come to the Logistics office, then staff record the loan with you.
- While Self-Service is open and any are available, the sheet first offers "At the office? Record it yourself.", with:
  - what Self-Service will ask;
  - a link, "Borrow it with Self-Service" (or "Take it with Self-Service"), to `/self-service?do=item&item=<id>`.
- The open sheet is in the address as `?item=<id>`:
  - Back closes it and returns focus to its row;
  - a link reopens it;
  - an id no longer listed is dropped from the address.
- Opening a sheet asks the API for nothing; the picture is the only extra request.
- `GET /api/public/catalog` adds `selfService: "open" | "paused"`. A real change of the Self-Service setting moves the catalog revision (`BUMP_REVISION … AND changes() > 0`), so an open Lending Hub learns of it on its next poll. A save that changes nothing moves nothing.

**Deviations from LH-LEND-01 §3, recorded in the spec:**
- The hand-off names the action ("Borrow it with Self-Service") rather than "Open in Self-Service".
- It sits above the in-person steps, so borrowing is the first thing under the item.

### A reason for Individual use

- Individual use asks for its specific reason, like USC use, at the desk (Lend) and on the phone (Self-Service). The field is always shown, with an example that fits the use.
- The Worker refuses a desk loan without one: "Enter what it will be used for."
- On the phone, the form says "Say what it's for." The check before sending reads "Individual use: <reason>".
- The Worker still accepts a phone borrow without a reason (`individualReasonOptional`), because a phone may hold a borrow saved offline before this update.

### Data Privacy Waiver Statement

Every Self-Service form (Borrow, Take, Use and Return) now carries the statement, because each one sends a name, a student ID and a photo. Its words are Earl's document exactly (spec item 9).
- **On the form:** the title, the opening paragraph and "Read full statement". The full statement opens in a sheet over the form, and closing it leaves the form as it was.
- **Consent:** "Yes. I hereby grant consent to the HAU-USC." is unticked on every new form; "No, I would not give my consent to the HAU-USC." takes it back.
- **Before consent:** the Review button looks as if it is waiting and says so to a screen reader. Pressing it still checks the form, with consent checked last. Not you? Change clears consent.
- **The check:** Check before you send shows "Data privacy: Consent given".
- **Limit:** the Worker does not store consent (that needs a migration). It is required on the phone, and no record reaches the check without it.

### The rail, the quantity editor and the picture

- **Rail:**
  - Items' fold chevron is its own button inside the Items plate, at its end (Items stays a link). It points down while folded and up while open, and each press folds or opens the group.
  - Folding eases out over 320 ms without overshoot, and the rows fade and lift as they go.
  - The current page is marked by lift, a lighter fill and the top highlight only. There is no gold edge on the wide rail and no gold ring on the icon rail, now or in any later rail design.
- **Quantity editor:** the figure sits centred between its two steps, with its unit and the change on lines of their own beneath, on the item sheet and in Stock.
- **Picture:** a cleaned picture's pedestal no longer shows a "Background removed" badge. The photo control's accessible name still says so.
