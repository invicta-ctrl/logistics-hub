## What was wrong

A read-only audit on 2026-10-29, checked by a second reviewer trying to refute it, found three writes that change what a revisioned list shows without moving the catalog revision. A page holding the list's tag asked again, got 304 and kept showing the old answer.

- **Keep both.** `keepBoth` in `src/ambient-assist.ts` removed the possible-duplicate finding and audited it, but left the revision alone. After Keep both the page refreshed Attention, got 304, and kept the card and the bell's count. Attention's tag also carries the hour, so this lasted until the hour turned or something else changed the catalog.
- **A new possible duplicate.** `recheckCapturedPhoto` writes its finding after the photo is saved; in production this runs after the upload has answered. A page that refreshed Attention between the two held a tag the new card never changed, so the card stayed hidden in the same way.
- **A renamed account.** `updateSelf` (My account) and `updateAccount` (Administration) changed `display_name` without moving the revision. Loans, Stock (restock entries) and Kits (last checked by) show staff by name under that revision's tag, so they kept the old name until the next catalog change of any kind.

The existing tests read Attention directly (`attention(env.DB)`), not through the Worker and its tag, so they could not see this.

## What changed

- `keepBoth` moves the revision in the same batch as the delete and its audit entry, only when the finding was really removed (`changes() > 0`, as in `src/attention.ts`).
- `recheckCapturedPhoto` writes its finding and moves the revision in one batch, only when the finding is new; a repeat that `INSERT OR IGNORE` skips moves nothing.
- `updateSelf` and `updateAccount` move the revision in their batch when the display name changes. Saving the same name, or changing only the username, role, access or active state, moves nothing.
