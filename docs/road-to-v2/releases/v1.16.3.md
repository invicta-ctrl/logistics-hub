## What changed for staff and visitors

- **One page frame.** Every staff page has a 48/56 Newsreader title (36/44 on phones) with one lede line, its actions level with the lede, and at most one primary action.
- **Lists:**
  - Views are quiet tabs with plain counts.
  - Search is a pill, with one Filters button at every width; on Items it opens on arrival when the address already narrows the list.
  - Items, Stock, places, kits, loans and Activity are rows on rules instead of boxed tables, with a chevron on hover where a row opens something.
  - Empty states sit on the page; figures sit on one ruled line.
- **Pages:**
  - Stock: Update stock is a sheet at every width.
  - Activity: grouped by day, each entry with its time, module icon and sentence.
  - My account: password and profile in the main column; sessions and the owner recovery key beside them.
  - Administration › System: its checks are a ruled list under the one status line.
- **Phones:**
  - Items rows are the picture, the name with its ID and kind, and the quantity over the status.
  - A sheet's close button keeps its corner beside a long title, and a sheet opens without a focus ring on it.
  - Sheets can be swiped down to close; unsaved changes still ask first.
  - Home's reasons are one sideways row, with Filters and the count on one line; queue rows drop the repeated "Open the item" link (guarded actions stay).
  - The pedestal's note becomes a corner badge on a small tile, and the place lines get room.
- **An item's picture lives in one place:**
  - Pressing the picture, or its icon before there is a photo, opens one menu: add or change the photo, crop, background, choose an icon, remove. On a phone it is a bottom action sheet.
  - "Take photo" goes, because the phone's own picker already offers the camera.
  - The icon and photo-or-icon choices move from Review & edit to an Item picture sheet opened from that menu.
- **Lending Hub and Self-Service:** pictures take their own background, and background-removed photos stand on a pedestal there too.
