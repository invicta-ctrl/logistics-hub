## What shipped

- **Administration → Staff Directory** (`/staff/admin/directory`), beside "Accounts & settings". People are grouped by the council's ten offices and departments in its own order (Office of the President, Vice President, Secretary-General; Finance, Events Management, Logistics, Community Extension Services, Public Communications, Human Resources, Business Relations), with professional names instead of folder labels and one quiet tint per department. Search (names, positions, sign-ins), a department index, and filters for officers, people without a USC ID, people not linked to a sign-in, and inactive people, each with a live count.
- **The directory is a wall of cards** (Earl, 2026-10-03: the card is what you press to see everything). Each person is an upright ID-style card drawn from their directory record (department colour, initials, name, position, Officer foil, ID-on-file and sign-in marks), never from a scan. Pressing one flies it out in 3D and turns it over to their details: student number, sign-in, USC ID, status, what is on loan now, and links to Profile, Usage, Loans and Activity. When a USC ID is on file, the same card turns on to its Front and Back (D/F/B); a scan is fetched, and the opening recorded, only then. Ctrl/⌘-press opens the full profile in a new tab; on a profile, the card beside the name opens straight on the ID.
- **The wall shows the IDs themselves** (Earl, 2026-10-03). Each person with an uploaded USC ID appears on the wall as their ID's front. Pressed, the card opens on **Profile**, the front and back together, and turns to **Details**. The person's photo, cut from a fixed place on the back of the 2026–27 USC ID, is their profile picture. The thumbnail and the picture are made in the owner's browser and stored beside the scans. Cards imported before this have none until the owner presses **Make them now** on the wall once. Neither picture is recorded one by one in Activity; opening the full scans still is.
- **Profiles: Profile | USC ID | Usage | Loans | Activity** (WAI-ARIA tabs, manual activation, the open section kept in the URL).
  - *Profile*: full name, department, position, officer status, optional student ID number, status, where the ID came from, the record's dates; edit with a stale-edit guard; the sign-in link.
  - *USC ID*: Front and Back, loaded only when this section opens. The card behaves like a holographic trading card in 3D (Earl, 2026-10-03, `src/card-motion.ts`):
    - the tiles lean toward the mouse;
    - opening flies the card out of its tile, turning once;
    - in the viewer it leans toward the pointer or a dragging finger under a glare and a holographic foil, presses in when held, and turns over on a spring (F/B);
    - closing flies it back into the tile of the side it shows.
    It rests flat and unlit, lies still while zoomed, and does none of this under reduced motion. Zoom 1–4× (wheel, pinch, double-click, + / − / Fit) and pan (drag, arrows). "Recent openings" sit beside it. The owner adds, replaces or removes the scans.
  - *Usage*: what left stock for the person (phone takes and loans) from the movement ledger, with dates, area, category and item filters and a summary.
  - *Loans*: what is out now, then returned and closed loans (Return works from here as anywhere).
  - *Activity*: what their linked sign-in did (the Activity feed narrowed to that account, with "Open in Activity"), and the directory record (added, edited, linked, imported, opened).
- **Owner import** from the downloaded Drive folder: the preflight runs on the device (names and hashes only), shows complete pairs, missing fronts and backs, duplicates, the same name in two departments, unknown department codes, malformed names, misfiled scans and skipped system files, and sends only complete, unambiguous pairs that are not already in the directory, one at a time, with progress, retry and a reconciliation summary.
- **Account linkage**: explicit, by stable internal ids, one person per sign-in and one sign-in per person, audited both ways. My account shows the signed-in person's directory entry.
- **Sign-in and access on the profile** (Earl, 2026-10-03: an easy way to create an account and monitor access). A person without a sign-in gets one in a step: the username is suggested from their name (`ana.santos`, then `ana.santos2`…), the role is limited to what the administrator may give, and a password is generated, shown once and must be replaced at first sign-in. The account and its link are written together only if the person still has no sign-in, and both are audited. Linking an existing sign-in is still offered. A linked person's profile shows whether the sign-in can be used, the last sign-in, how many devices are signed in now, failed sign-ins in the current 15-minute window (with a warning from three), the last ten sign-ins and how each ended, and the last ten changes to the account. Whoever manages the account can reset the password, sign it out everywhere or disable it from there. All of it comes from the existing account, session, throttle and audit tables: no migration, and no session id leaves the server.
- **Activity** gains the source "Staff Directory" (administrators and owners only).

## Data model, storage and import design

- **D1, migration `0021_staff_directory.sql` (additive only; the code live before it ignores both tables):**
  - `staff_directory`: `id` (`PER-<uuid>`), `full_name`, `department` (code, validated by the Worker against `src/directory-policy.ts`), `position`, `officer` (status beside the department), `student_id` (unique, optional, typed by a person), `active`, `account_id` (unique, references `staff_accounts`), `source_key` (unique: normalised identity + department code), created/updated at/by.
  - `staff_id_cards`: one card per person, `media_id` (random, new on every replacement), front and back dimensions, the two source file names, who and when. The only reference to the image objects.
- **R2:** the dedicated private bucket `logistics-hub-staff-ids`, binding `STAFF_IDS`, keys `ids/<media id>/front` and `ids/<media id>/back`. Never the catalog (`CATALOG_MEDIA`) or evidence (`EVIDENCE`) bucket. Images are browser-made JPEGs, at most 2000 px and 1.5 MB, rebuilt by the Worker without any metadata (the V1.2 JPEG check, generalised as `checkJpeg`).
- **Pairing (`src/staff-import.ts`):** a file is `<Identity>_<Front|Back>_<CODE>.<png|jpg|jpeg|webp>` in a department folder (`[CODE] Official ID`) or `OFFICERS`. The key is the normalised identity (accents, case and separators folded) + the department code from the file name + the side; never the surname alone. An `OFFICERS` file keeps the department its name encodes and sets officer status. Byte-identical copies (the same scan in a department folder and in `OFFICERS`) count once; different scans for one key and side are duplicates. The same name in two departments is held as ambiguous, not merged and not guessed. A file whose code differs from its department folder is held. Nothing is read from the image: no OCR, no faces.
- **Writes are ordered so D1 never points at a missing file:** both scans go to R2 first; one D1 batch adds the person (or finds them by source key) and attaches the card only if they have none, with its audit entry; a refused or failed write removes the new files unless D1 may already refer to them. Importing the same archive again changes nothing. Replacing and removing follow the V1.2 compare-and-swap pattern on `media_id`.
- **Usage and loans are read, not copied:** from `inventory_movements` joined to `loans` and `self_service_events`. A record belongs to a person when both carry a student ID and they are equal, or otherwise when its name is exactly the person's full name (case aside). A one-word name never matches by name, so an import that starts from a surname shows no one else's records.
- The 0001 table `staff_users` is a different concept and stays as it is (Earl, 2026-10-03). It holds the authorized DOL staff, filled privately by `scripts/build-private-staff-seed.mjs`, and was empty in production when checked read-only on 2026-10-03. The directory is for looking people up and tracking usage, and grants nothing. V1.3 neither reads nor writes `staff_users`. An earlier V1.3 merge (`57d62a9`) had declared it retired and removed the seed script, following the doctrine's first framing; that was undone.

### Independent review and what it changed

A read-only reviewer read the whole V1.3 diff and found no authorization bypass, injection, XSS or leak route. Its findings were each verified in the code and fixed, with tests where the Worker is involved:

- **Re-openings could go unrecorded:** the page kept a scan in memory for the whole visit, so opening the same card again after ten minutes sent no request and wrote no Activity entry. A kept scan is now fetched again after five minutes, so every opening outside the ten-minute window is recorded; a person's scans are also dropped as soon as another person's profile opens.
- **Listeners piled up** on the directory page (each re-render added another "Add person"/"Import" handler) and in the Loans section (each return stacked another return dialog). Both are bound once now.
- **A half-stored card:** if R2 failed on the back after storing the front, the front stayed behind. Both sides are now removed on any failure (test).
- **An administrator could rename or deactivate the directory entry of someone linked to an administrator or owner sign-in**, which is that person's verified identity, though they could not link or unlink it. Editing such a profile now follows the linking rule (test).
- **An upload without a length** was read in full before being refused; the scan routes now require `Content-Length` (411 otherwise; browsers always send it) (test).
- **The per-viewer limit** is lowered from 240 to 120 scan requests in ten minutes (about 60 cards), so bulk viewing stops sooner; every opening is recorded either way.
- Not changed, recorded below: an import finds people only by its own key, so someone added by hand before an import of their scan appears twice.
- CI green on both pushed code checkpoints (runs 37089670320, 37091183076).

## Known limitations

- **The real import is not done** (owner action 4). In the cloud the importer checked the Drive listing only; the scans were viewed for the identity check, but this code has not decoded their pixels yet. If a real scan cannot be decoded by the browser (an unusual PNG), that pair fails with a message and can be retried or re-saved; nothing partial is stored.
- **The scan source is small today**: two DEM pairs. Everyone in the directory sheet has an entry (owner action 5), but the other departments' scans and the `OFFICERS` folder are not in Drive yet; when they are, the importer attaches them to the existing entries by file name.
- **Profile pictures are initials.** A portrait cut from the ID would need finding the face (excluded) or a fixed crop of the card template that has not been seen on real scans; the USC ID section shows the card itself, only when opened, with the opening logged.
- **Name matching is exact.** Loans and phone records written with another spelling, or USC-use records typed by name only when the person's full name differs, do not appear; the page says how records were found. Adding the student ID number gives the most reliable match.
- **Administrators can open ID scans** (audited and rate-limited); only the owner changes them. A stricter rule (owner-only viewing) is one line in `idScan` if Earl wants it.
- **Linking does not change other workflows.** No existing screen asks the signed-in person to type their own name or ID (the actor always comes from the session), so there was nothing to prefill; My account shows the linked entry.
- **Import and hand-added people:** an import recognises people only by its key (normalised file name + department). Someone added by hand first (with a full name) and then imported from a scan appears twice; merge by hand (add the scans to the hand-made entry with USC ID → Add ID scans, then make the imported one inactive and remove its scans). Re-importing never changes officer status or names someone has edited.
- **Departments are a fixed list in code** (`src/directory-policy.ts`); a new office needs a code change, not a migration.
- **Activity CSV export** (owner/admin) carries directory sentences with the person's name and department, like account events carry usernames; it never carries a student ID number.
- **Not tested on real devices or screen readers**; pinch-zoom was exercised by code paths and keyboard, not on a phone.
- **The 3D card is decorative and has limits.** It never changes what a scan shows at rest. The phone's motion sensor is not used: iOS asks a permission to read it, and dragging tilts instead. Its blend modes cost some GPU time on low-end phones. Frame times were measured only in headless Chromium with software rendering (median 16.7 ms, no long tasks), not on a real phone. A turning card can briefly cover the viewer's top bar for about 150 ms.
- **Research provenance**: sources were read through search-result summaries (see `docs/visual-research/v1.3.md`).
