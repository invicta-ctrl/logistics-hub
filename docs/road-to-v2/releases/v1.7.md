## What shipped

- **Check a place, in the Catalog.** The Catalog's home has **Check a place**: choose a place and **Start checking**. The list is every active item recorded there and in the places inside it, with how many the records expect. Resume your open or paused checks from the home screen on any device; see who else is checking (one check per place at a time).
- **Three answers per item:** **Here · N** (the expected count, stated on the button), **Count differs** (a stepper), **I can’t find it**; and, behind ⋯, **Record looks wrong** with a note (the spec's review-needed state; amendment §11). **Found something not on the list** records **Found here** (recorded elsewhere, picked from the saved catalog, with how many) or **Not in the catalog** (with a name).
- **Progress and findings:** "7 / 12 checked · 3 findings", a progress bar, To check / Checked / Findings. The place's directions are at hand, with a note when its directions or picture need fixing (kept on the check and shown in the summary).
- **Pause and resume** (explicitly, or by marking something), across reloads, devices and the connection.
- **Offline under V1.6's model.** A check starts, runs, pauses and finishes offline (under the device's offline access); the expected list comes from the saved catalog. Everything marked is written on the device first (store version 3) and sent in order by V1.6's sender: each once, a second look never before the first, a refresh never losing what wasn't sent.
- **Completion: a summary and a discrepancy review, never silent stock changes.** Finishing changes nothing. The summary (who, when, how long; expected, checked, here, findings, not checked; the place note) prints for handover. Signed in, each finding is settled through what already guards that change: **Post count** (the ledger's count movement, keyed to the observation, the check named in its note), **Move it here** (the bulk Move, audited), **Report its location** (a location report), or **Leave it as it is** with a reason. A count overtaken by stock that moved since ("Stock changed since this was counted") is counted again and posted against the figure on screen.
- **Freshness, derived:** an item's profile says when it was last seen at its place in a check and last counted, and links to any finding still to settle; a place says when it was last checked. Nothing is stored for these: they are read from the checks and the ledger.
- **Activity:** checks started, paused, resumed, finished and findings settled appear under Catalog, worded for people.

## Discrepancy and ledger evidence

- **Observations never move stock.** Worker test: six outcomes recorded and the check finished, and every item's on hand, movement count and place are unchanged. End to end on a real Worker and D1: the same across an online, paused and offline check.
- **The only stock change is the ledger's own count, deliberately.** Worker test: settling a count differs posts one `COUNT_ADJUSTMENT` (signed change −2, idempotency key `audit-<observation>`), a repeat posts nothing more, and the observation still reads as counted. End to end: the posted count names "the check of E2E V17 Store › E2E V17 Shelf" in its note.
- **Stale counts are refused, not posted.** A count made against figures that were already stale when it arrived (offline while stock moved) or overtaken afterwards is refused (409, "Count it again"); a fresh count against the figure on screen posts. The ledger's `expectedOnHand` guard backs this in the database for every writer.
- **A count is only posted for an item checked at its own place** (count differs, can't find): a few found on another shelf say nothing about the rest (400).
- **Evidence is kept.** Observations and resolutions are append-only (triggers refuse UPDATE and DELETE); a finished check refuses more observations and cannot reopen; a check is never deleted. A later count supersedes a finding in the freshness view while the observation stays as it was seen (Worker test).

## Offline and conflict evidence

- **Exactly once.** Browser: two marks made offline survive a reload and are sent once each on reconnecting. End to end (real service worker): three marks made offline (one found here, one not in the catalog) wait on the device, survive a reload, and arrive once each; then every answer is lost on purpose (the server saves, the device never hears) and the resend is kept once.
- **Conflicts kept for a person.** Each observation stores the figure the device showed and the server's on arrival; the review flags any difference, or a movement since, and asks for a fresh count. Someone else checking a place when a device's offline start arrives: what was seen there stops for a person and is never filed under the other check.
- **Order.** Within a check the sender stops at the first observation that must wait, so the server's "latest observation" is always the latest seen.
- **Lease scope.** On a device's offline access alone, a member may start, read, observe, pause and finish their own checks; reading someone else's check is 403; the review and settling are 401 (a full sign-in is required to change stock).

## Permission notes

Any signed-in Logistics staff member can check a place and settle any finished check's findings, as they can already record counts and move items; staff of other departments and officers get 403 as for every Hub route. Only the person checking can add to, pause or finish their check; anyone with Hub access can read it. On a device's offline access alone, a member can work only on their own checks and cannot settle anything.

## Known limitations

- Real phones and screen readers were not tested; the evidence is Chromium with device emulation.
- Offline, the expected list and its figures are the saved catalog's: stock that moved since it was saved is caught at the review (count again), not while checking.
- Place pictures are not shown offline (V1.6: thumbnails are not saved on the device); directions are.
- A check covers a place and up to eight levels inside it, at most 2,000 expected items (a whole storeroom); "Not on the list" searches the saved catalog by name and other names only.
- Found here can be moved or reported but not counted from the check; count it in a check of its own place.
- One check per place at a time: a second person sees who is checking and checks another place.
