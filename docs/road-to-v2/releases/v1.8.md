## What shipped

- **Kits** (`/staff/kits`, staff only): a name, optional picture, description, place, active flag and a list of components, each an existing item with how many the kit should hold. One kit can mix borrow & return, Take and use-gradually items; each keeps its own rules. A kit holds no stock.
- **Derived states, never stored:** **Ready**, **Needs replenishment** (a component is short, running low or expiring within 30 days) or **Needs review** (a component is retired, unverified, unclassified, or was missing or damaged at the last check; or the kit is empty). Each non-ready component says why in a sentence. Stock one item pool cannot cover for two kits at once counts as short for both.
- **Kit Templates:** make one by hand or from a kit; making a kit from a template copies its list and nothing else. The kit is not linked afterwards; each physical kit has its own name, place and state.
- **Check kit:** one answer per component (All there, Running low, Missing, Damaged), an optional note, **Mark everything not yet marked as all there**, then a summary. A check is append-only (database triggers refuse update and delete), resent checks are found by their id, and it writes no movement. The summary says "Stock was not changed by this check" and links each finding to its item.
- **Expiry and reorder** come from the components' own records (expiry date, reorder level, open item condition).
- **Catalog:** an item's profile says which kits it is in, linking to them; building a kit offers the items just put on the shelf by Rapid Catalogue ("Recently catalogued").
- **Activity** words kit creation, edits, checks, pictures and templates.
- **Catalog intelligence amendment (§11):** "Consume" is now **Take** and "Consumed or used" is **Taken or used** (label constants in `src/catalog-policy.ts`, tests and copy updated). The built-in item knowledge base (`src/item-knowledge.ts`, version 1, 28 entries). Suggestions now carry a strength tier, Strong, Weak, Conflicting or None, and no percentages anywhere; the capture screen says **Suggested** for Strong, **Maybe** for weaker, and shows both options with their reasons for a Conflicting field, which "Use these" never fills. The leave-one-out script (`npm run evaluate:suggestions`). The container/contents pattern is documented.
- Self-Service never shows a kit (tested). Kit routes are not in the offline lease allow-list, so they need a full sign-in.

## Knowledge base and item-icons.ts

The two files answer different questions: `item-icons.ts` says what an item looks like, `item-knowledge.ts` says how it is counted, taken and filed (behaviour, unit, category). The knowledge base carries no icon key and never repeats a whole icon list (enforced by `tests/item-knowledge.test.ts`). 41 single words appear in both (ribbon, bleach, spoon…) because they are the catalog's own vocabulary; neither file derives from the other, and the knowledge base is not a copy of the icon lists. It is imported by `catalogue-suggest.ts`, which both staff pages and the Catalog PWA bundle, so it works offline.

## Suggestion evaluation (amendment §9; aggregates only)

Leave-one-out over the 254 confirmed items of the repository's public seed catalog (`npm run evaluate:suggestions`). The seed's behaviour labels are noisy, so read the shape, not the absolute figures. "Right" is the first choice matching the held-out item's real value.

|---|---|---|
| Behaviour | 87 (76% right) / 93 (69%) / 6 | 81 (75%) / 92 (67%) / 14 (either option right 93%) |
| Unit | 78 (92%) / 88 (81%) / 20 | 65 (94%) / 89 (80%) / 38 (either option right 89%) |
| Category | 106 (93%) / 79 (85%) / 1 | 103 (93%) / 77 (84%) / 13 (either option right 100%) |

Suggested for: behaviour 73% → 74%, unit 73% → 76%, category 73% → 76%. Strong needs an exact name match or 4 agreeing look-alikes (`STRONG_VOTES`, raised from 3 after measuring: 4 separates Strong from Weak in every field) with no knowledge-base disagreement. The knowledge base turns some silent disagreements into Conflicting, which is shown to the person as two options instead of one confident guess. It was not tuned on, and is not evaluated against, any private catalog.
