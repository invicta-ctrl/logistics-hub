## What shipped

- **Logistics Catalog, an installable staff app.** `public/catalogue.webmanifest` (id and start `/staff/catalogue`, scope `/staff`, its own icons: the DOL mark on a cream disc over dark oxblood). The page links the manifest (and iOS touch icon) of the app its route belongs to (`installableAs` in `main.ts`). Sign-in accepts `?next=` back to the Catalogue.
- **Self-Service keeps its own install.** Its manifest's scope narrows from `/` to `/self-service` (same id; installed phones update in place as the same app), so the two apps' scopes do not overlap: nested scopes on one site make an installed outer app claim the inner app's pages and hide its install. All Self-Service screens, its start URL and its shortcuts were already under `/self-service`.
- **Offline cataloguing on a device.** While signed in, **Turn on offline cataloguing** gives the device a week-long lease (below), saves the Catalogue's screens (service worker) and a minimal copy of the catalog (`GET /api/staff/catalogue/snapshot`), and asks the browser to keep the site's data. The card says **Ready for offline cataloguing**, until when, and when the catalog was saved, or what is missing.
- **Offline, the V1.5 session works as online:** open the Catalogue (from the saved page), start or resume a session, capture with photos, finish. Suggestions and possible matches read the same saved catalog online and offline, so they answer the same (amendment §11). New places need a connection.
- **Queued, clearly marked, sent once.** Captures wait on the device (`catalogue-store.ts`, the V1.5 outbox grown to IndexedDB v2) as **Not saved yet** / **N waiting to send** (the same words as V1.5's dropped connection). `catalogue-sync.ts` sends one member's work in order: a session started offline under the id the device proposed, each capture and photo, then a finish made offline. Captures of a session finished on another device go to a new session. A repeat never makes a second item, session or photo.
- **Updates keep work and readiness.** Queued work lives in IndexedDB, which updates never touch; every new service-worker version saves the Catalogue's screens again on a device with offline cataloguing on. Updates wait until the capture form is empty.
- **No migration.** Leases are `staff_sessions` rows with a `CL-` id.

## Security model

- **No password on the device.** The lease is a `staff_sessions` row whose id starts `CL-`, in its own cookie `lh_catalogue_lease` (`HttpOnly; SameSite=Strict; Path=/api/staff/; Secure` on HTTPS; 7 days), signed with its own key. A lease cannot pass as a session, nor a session as a lease (each is verified with its own key and must name its own kind of row).
- **Only cataloguing.** Without a full session, a lease may: read the Catalogue page's own open session (no one else's, no Review later list), its own sessions' detail, the snapshot and its own lease state; start a session, change its place, capture, finish; add the **first** photo to an item **its own sessions** catalogued; turn itself off. Every other staff route answers 401 as for no sign-in (tested route by route), and another member's session answers 403.
- **Ends predictably.** Sign out on the device; **Turn off offline cataloguing**; sign out everywhere; a password change or reset; a change of role or access; deactivation; owner recovery; or expiry. Turning it on or off is audited (`CATALOGUE_OFFLINE_ON` / `_OFF`, under Accounts in Activity and the security log). One lease per device: turning it on where another member's lease was left ends that one.
- **What the device keeps** (`docs/OFFLINE_CATALOGUE.md` §5): its own captures and photos until sent, the sessions it works on, who the lease belongs to and until when, and the snapshot (items' names, kinds, identity signals, places and on hand; categories; units; the place tree). No notes, loans, borrowers, history, audit or other staff. No USC IDs or private evidence. Nothing personal in Cache Storage.
- **Every route** added or opened to a lease sits behind the existing same-origin, Logistics-access (`hubAccess`) and must-change-password checks.

## Offline lease behaviour

| Situation | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Signed in, online | Normal. The lease is moved a full week ahead (at most once a day) whenever the Catalogue opens. |
| Sign-in ended, lease valid | "You're signed out. This device can still catalogue for you until …"; cataloguing and sending continue on the lease; Items, Stock and the rest need a sign-in. |
| Offline, lease valid | "You're offline. You can keep cataloguing on this device until …"; everything is saved on the device and sent when the connection is back. |
| Offline, no lease (never on, turned off, ended) | "The Catalogue needs a connection here", with what waits on the device. |
| Lease ended while work waits | Nothing is deleted. Sending stops at the first 401; the work is sent after the member's next sign-in on the device. |
| Another member's work on a shared device | Listed and counted; only that member's sign-in sends it; Discard only after a confirm. |

## Known limitations

- First use needs a connection and a full sign-in; iPhone and iPad catalogue offline only from the Home Screen app.
- Offline, a session open on another device is not known; one started here joins it when sent (one open session per person), and finishing it here then finishes that one too.
- New places need a connection and a full sign-in.
- A capture made offline is timed when it reaches the server.
- Thumbnails of items already on the server are not shown offline or on a lease.
- Browser data cleared before sending loses that work (the guides warn).
- Real devices and screen readers were not tested.
- Locally on Windows, `tests/production-release.test.ts` fails (a CRLF copy of a pinned migration in its throwaway repo) and two older end-to-end tests that count short-lived toasts fail under heavy machine load; the same happens on the last commit that passed before, and Linux CI passes them.

## Follow-up: install and design fixes (2026-10-04)

Earl reported two defects after V1.6 went live.

1. **Installing from the Catalogue on an iPhone gave a second Self-Service.** The Catalogue was served from `index.html`, which links Self-Service's manifest, and script re-pointed the manifest after load (`installableAs`). Chromium followed the change, so the DevTools install check passed; iOS Safari installs from the manifest the page loaded with. **Fixed:** the Catalog has its own page, `staff/catalogue.html` (second Vite entry, served by the Worker's assets at `/staff/catalogue`), with its manifest, touch icon and `apple-mobile-web-app-title` in the HTML. The router loads the other app's page when moving between the Catalog and the rest (`main.ts`, at most once per route, never offline); `installableAs` and `forgetInstallPrompt` are gone. The service worker saves the Catalog's page with the Catalogue's files and opens it offline. Self-Service, its manifest, scope and id are unchanged.
2. **The Catalogue, offline above all, wore the staff workspace's bar and sections** (Items, Stock, Loans… that cannot open offline) instead of a look of its own, as Self-Service has. **Fixed:** `catalogueShell()` (`src/catalogue-shell.ts`, `src/catalogue.css`) gives the Catalog its own frame: its name and mark (as on its icon), a connection light (**Online** / **Offline** / **Signed out**), an account menu (Staff workspace and Sign out; Sign in on a lease; offline, a note), and on home a dark band with the heading and the offline or signed-out note, the first card overlapping it. Copy is unchanged apart from where it sits. Research, choices and the five defects found by looking: `docs/visual-research/v1.6.md` ("Follow-up").

Staff who already added the wrong icon on an iPhone remove it and add the Catalog again (`docs/PWA_INSTALL_GUIDE.md`, Troubleshooting). Engineering: `docs/OFFLINE_CATALOGUE.md` §2, §3, §7.

No migration, no new dependency, no server route changed; `worker.ts` is untouched (the Worker's assets already serve `staff/catalogue.html` at `/staff/catalogue`, checked on a local Worker).
