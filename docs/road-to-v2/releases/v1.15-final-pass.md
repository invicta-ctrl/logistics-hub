## What changed

- **CatalogDraft (FP-B/FP-C).** `src/catalog-draft.ts`: one typed proposal for every Add items field, each with its source (person, session, verified catalogue, knowledge base, photo, default) and state (verified, suggested, needs confirmation, unknown). The person's entry always wins and a retake, reset or catalogue change drops older results. Quantity is never taken from a photo; serial, expiry, reorder level and notes are never guessed. The screen says "Count what is on the shelf; a photo cannot show the real amount." and "Still to choose: …" only for fields nothing was offered for. The line is a polite status region that is announced once; opening and leaving the count field settles it.
- **Gemma photo reading (FP-C).** `src/ambient-assist.ts`: name, brand, model and packaging as printed, each checked on its own (length, characters, instruction-like text refused). The Model field is prefilled and labelled "Read from the photo. Check it, or type over it." Truncated replies are salvaged by name only. Cost: 3.94 Neurons for one call, 4.13 at the largest allowed photo; reserve 6.
- **Three text roles (FP-D).** `src/ai-roles.ts`: Granite (normalize), Qwen (arbitrate) and GLM (rare second opinion) keep their per-role reserves, token limits, timeouts, breakers and Neuron bands. The accepted review-only amendment now starts all three in `REVIEW_ONLY`: a strict allowlisted answer becomes a typed human proposal with null trusted value and observation. The 2026-10-10 continuation wires them to explicit, audited staff review; `scripts/evaluate-roles.mjs --dry` is deterministic and makes zero provider calls.
- **Picture cleanup (FP-E).** `src/item-cutout.ts`: Cloudflare Images `segment=foreground` on the stored display photo through the `IMAGES` binding; result kept as a transparent PNG beside the original (`items/{id}/cutout`); "Remove background" and "Use original" on the item's photo panel; audit entries; the cutout goes with its photo. Safeguards: the owner's switch (default off), a 500 a month cap counted in `system_settings`, a breaker (3 failures, 10 minutes), a 20 s timeout, an already-cleaned photo is never sent again, and a 4 MB, 1,600 px and proportions check on what comes back. The Worker reads only the PNG's headers; a new cut is stored as **pending**, which is not the item's picture, and **the browser judges its transparency from the alpha channel, then accepts it or deletes it** (`src/cutout-share.ts`), so a closed tab or failed request leaves the original in use and nothing bad live. Judging happens in the browser because decoding the pixels in the Worker took 50 to 120 ms of CPU against Workers Free's 10 ms. The cleaned picture is served `private, no-cache` since it can be made again under the same id. Staff on an offline lease cannot use it. There is no face or person check on item photos (none existed before; the app only asks staff to show the item itself).
