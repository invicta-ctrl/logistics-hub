# V1.15 Final Pass: text-role benchmark, 2026-10-08

Method: `scripts/evaluate-roles.mjs` (helpers in `src/role-evaluation.ts`, tested). Synthetic, deterministic variants (typo, dropped word, reordered words, plural) of the repository's own public seed catalogue (394 distinct names, 1,272 variants). No photo, no private data. The truth is the original name. Same candidate lists for the baseline and the model. Gate used: precision of shown answers at least 95%, at least 30 answers, more fixes than breaks (amendment §7.2 targets, applied by me; Earl has not set different numbers). Run through the REST endpoint on the confirmed account, inside Earl's 13:43 UTC authorization for bounded Free-tier calls.

Deterministic baseline (trigram closest name, answers only when clearly ahead): resolves 1,021 of 1,272 variants, precision 99%. A model is only useful on the 251 it leaves.

| Role | Cases | Answered | Precision | Fixes / breaks vs baseline | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Granite, 40 candidate terms | 120 baseline-unresolved variants | 120 | 52% (58 wrong) | 62 / 0 | FAIL quality gate |
| Granite, after one remediation pass (stricter "answer only if clearly the same item" prompt, 8 closest terms) | 120 | 120 | 55% (54 wrong) | 66 / 0 | FAIL quality gate |
| Qwen, 5 near-tied candidates | 80 | 80 | 38% (50 wrong); baseline nearest-name 40% | 0 / 2 | FAIL quality gate |
| GLM, fixed conflict codes | 10 | 10 | not scoreable (follow-up questions) | n/a | conformance only: CHECK_CATEGORY 5, CHECK_NAME 5 |

Cost and speed: 210 calls then 120 more; about 187 Neurons in total (148.1 + 39.1) across both runs; latency p50 about 0.6 s, p95 about 1.1 s; no failed call.

Failure taxonomy (first look, not exhaustive): models rarely answer null, so they pick a sibling item when the typed text fits several ("Heavy duty" with the last word dropped matches many stapler names); the set is hard by construction (only variants the baseline could not resolve); precision stays near a coin flip with fewer distractors, so candidate count is not the main cause.

Decision: Granite, Qwen and GLM stay in SHADOW_EVALUATION. None is promoted to a field. The adapters, budgets and breakers remain, as the amendment requires. Remediation still to try (needs a decision, not more spend): require agreement with the deterministic top-2 before a model answer can be shown, or use the model only to rank candidates for a person (review triage), which the amendment allows as "better review triage". Real held-out USC cases and photo-name accuracy for Gemma are not measured here and remain unverified.

## Second run: corrected harness, tuning half (2026-10-08, 77.2 Neurons)

Review findings E1 to E4 and E6 changed the harness before this run: the catalogue is split once by a fixed hash into a TUNE half (204 names) and a sealed TEST half (190 names); only TUNE was used here. Variants that more than one catalogue name could explain (75 on the tuning half) are set aside, not scored. Plausible names that are NOT in the catalogue are asked too, since the right answer for them is no answer. Coverage prints beside precision. Calls and Neurons have ceilings no flag can lift (300 calls, 400 Neurons, stop after 5 consecutive failures). Printed summary: `v1.15-final-pass-role-benchmark-tune-run-2026-10-08.txt`.

| Role | Cases | Coverage | Precision | Answered where no name was right | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Granite, 40 terms (baseline silent on all) | 77 (25 not in the catalogue) | 100% | 61% (30 wrong) | 25 of 25 | FAIL |
| Qwen, 5 near-tied candidates (baseline = nearest name, precision 45%) | 29 (15 not in the catalogue) | 97% | 46% | 14 of 15 | FAIL (and under the 30-answer support line) |
| GLM, fixed conflict codes | 6 | n/a | conformance only | n/a | CHECK_CATEGORY 3, CHECK_NAME 3 |

Reading it: on real, resolvable variants Granite is right 47 of 52 times (90%, still under 95%); the failure is that **neither model ever abstains**. Asked about a name that is not in the list, Granite picked a catalogue name 25 times out of 25 and Qwen 14 out of 15. A role that cannot say "none of these" cannot fill a field safely, so the verdict stands: all three stay in `SHADOW_EVALUATION`. The deterministic baseline (precision 99%, 530 of 603 variants) is what staff see.

Net benefit (fixes against breaks) is not used for Granite: the baseline was silent on every case it was asked, so breaks are zero by construction (the earlier "62 / 0" was vacuous, as the review said). Qwen's 0 / 0 is real: it never beat the nearest name.

The sealed TEST half has not been run and will not be run until a role's prompt or gating is frozen for a promotion claim. These REST calls do not write the app's daily Neuron counter; across both benchmark runs, the smokes and the probes the account spent roughly 270 Neurons outside it, well under the 6,500 band.

Open decision for Earl (no spend needed): keep the three roles as measured shadows (recommended), or ask for the triage design (a model orders candidates for a person, which the amendment allows) as follow-up work after the final pass merges.

Harness note (review on PR 29): the ceiling was first checked only against Neurons already reported, so a run near the line could finish above it, and a failed call counted as zero. It now refuses a call unless the role's conservative reserve fits in what is left, and a failed call keeps its reserve. Both recorded runs stayed far under their ceilings (77.2 of 250 and 67.3 of 150 Neurons), so no run was affected.
