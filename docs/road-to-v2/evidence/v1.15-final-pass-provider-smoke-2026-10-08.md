### CPU cost of judging the cut (reviewer finding H1)

Decoding a PNG's pixels in the Worker to measure its transparency took 50 to 120 ms of CPU for a 1280 × 960 cutout in Node (about 5 ms even at 320 × 240), against the 10 ms a Workers Free request gets. A request killed after the provider call is counted would fail every cleanup and still spend allowance. The Worker therefore reads only the PNG's chunk headers (under 1 ms) and the browser judges transparency from the canvas, undoing a bad cut at once.
